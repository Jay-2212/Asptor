Uttar Pradesh Assembly has 403 seats. The second round of discussions between the SP and Congress on seat-sharing is underway

By Mayank Yadav • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-24

Seat-sharing negotiations between the Samajwadi Party (SP) and Congress continued on Tuesday (November 24, 2026) in New Delhi, with additional rounds of talks expected to follow.

The SP initially proposed setting aside 26 seats for Congress, which was rejected by the party, as per sources.

While speculation continues over seat allocations and bargaining between both sides, leaders of both the parties maintained that constructive negotiations are continuing and a deal will be sealed in the coming days.

In a post on X, SP president Akhilesh Yadav said a beginning had been made with the first 26 “winnable seats”, adding that further discussions would move forward through mutual understanding and cooperation.

“Started together with winnable ‘first 26 seats’, things will move forward with cooperation and mutual understanding,” he wrote on X. 

The talks continued for over three hours.

Before going for the meeting, SP General Secretary and Rajya Sabha MP Ram Gopal Yadav proposed giving 26 seats to the Congress party.

“Congress won two of these seats in the previous elections, while the party finished second in four. In the remaining 20 seats, the SP received 60,000 to 70,000 votes, while Congress received around 2,000. The SP is proposing to give these seats to the Congress party,” he told reporters outside the residence of Congress Rajya Sabha MP Pramod Tiwari, where the discussions were being held.

Later, Mr. Ramgopal Yadav said, “We discussed seats and the talks were held in a very positive manner. But 403 seats are a lot, isn’t it? So, the discussion has not been completed yet, but you can be assured that we will complete this alliance in the next two [rounds of] meetings.”

A Congress leader who took part in the negotiation process said the shared list of 26 seats was not acceptable for the party, and accordingly, the party asked the SP to hold seat-wise discussions on more seats.

“Congress is a national party, our primary aim is to defeat the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) decisively in Uttar Pradesh. But along with this, we want a respectable seat share in the alliance. We will hold discussions on every seat and put forth our claim on a portion of seats out of 403. Yesterday, we shared a list of 130-odd seats which we want to contest,” the leader said on condition of anonymity.

Later, Congress leaders held their own meeting in the evening to decide on the party’s strategy on seat sharing.

Speaking to reporters in Lucknow, SP chief Akhilesh Yadav said alliances are formed to win elections, adding that alliance parties had to work together and make adjustments in a way that accommodates each other.

“The discussions are being held by our party’s senior leaders, who have been entrusted with the responsibility. Talks are still under way, and it is being discussed how many seats each party can agree on and how many it can win. So, it is difficult to say anything at this stage. Alliances are formed to win elections. We must work together and make adjustments in a way that accommodates each other and helps us defeat our opponents. That is the biggest responsibility of political parties...,” he said.

Congress MP Imran Masood in New Delhi said, “We are discussing everything, not just the candidate for these seats. We are discussing a lot of things... The dream is a strong alliance on 403 seats.”

It is likely the discussions will be held in multiple phases after November 26 and it will take weeks for the SP-Congress to reach a clear understanding on seats. The two parties are willing to accommodate some smaller players in Uttar Pradesh, such as the left parties in the INDIA bloc, including Communist Party of India (Marxist–Leninist) Liberation.

The SP team consists of National General Secretary and MP Ram Gopal Yadav, Lok Sabha MPs Rajeev Rai and Iqra Choudhary and state party chief Shyam Lal Pal. On Tuesday (November 24, 2026), Awadhesh Prasad, representing Faizabad in the Lok Sabha on the SP symbol, also joined the SP team in the discussions.

The Congress team consists of the party’s U.P. in-charge Rajendra Pal Gautam, state party chief Aradhna Misra, working president Imran Masood, and MLA Virendra Chaudhary.
