Post-Deepavali smog envelopes cities as air quality plummets to 'severe' | In pictures

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-10

Thick smog enveloped several Indian cities on Monday (November 9, 2026), after firecrackers were burst late into Deepavali night on Sunday (November 8, 2026). 

Despite restrictions on bursting firecrackers, several cities continued to witness their use on Sunday (November 8, 2026) evening. As celebrations continued overnight, X users reported worsening conditions, noting a strong smell of smoke, poor visibility, and breathing difficulties.

Delhi’s air quality touched the ‘severe’ category, while Mumbai, Lucknow, Chennai and Hyderabad also recorded ‘poor’ or worse readings. 

Here’s a collection of pictures of the haze from across the country:

A thick layer of toxic haze engulfs Delhi as people commute near Rashtrapati Bhavan, shrouded in thick smog during the early morning of November 9, 2026, a day after Deepavali. The city’s 24-hour average AQI was 370, in the “very poor” category, at 9 a.m.

Photo: Shashi Shekhar Kashyap

People jog near India Gate, shrouded in thick smog, as Delhi’s AQI touched 404 in the ‘severe’ category at 4 a.m. on November 9, 2026, according to the CPCB’s live dashboard.
