2008 Mumbai terror attacks: Tributes paid to martyrs on 18th anniversary

By PTI • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-26

Maharashtra Governor Jishnu Dev Varma and Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis on Thursday (November 26, 2026) paid floral tributes at the city police headquarters to those who made the ultimate sacrifice while fighting terrorists during the Mumbai terror attacks 18 years ago.

Deputy Chief Minister Sunetra Pawar, Director General of Police Sadanand Date, Mumbai Police Commissioner Deven Bharti and other officials were present.

Mr. Varma and Mr. Fadnavis also met the family members of the policemen who sacrificed their lives during the 26/11 attacks.

“Humble tributes to the brave heroes who laid down their lives defending Mumbai against the 26/11 terror attacks and to the innocent civilians who lost their lives,” Mr*. Fadnavis said in a post on X.

The November 26, 2008, attacks, carried out by 10 heavily armed Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) terrorists who sailed into Mumbai from Pakistan, left 166 people, including foreigners, dead and more than 300 injured, making them one of the deadliest terror strikes in an Indian city.

An exterior view of the Trident hotel at Nariman Point on the eve of the 18th anniversary of the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks, in Mumbai. | Photo Credit: PTI

The attacks prompted global condemnation and led to significant changes in India’s counter-terrorism measures.

The attack, which continued until the morning of November 29, targeted several iconic locations bustling with people, including the Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT), the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, the Trident Hotel, Nariman House (Chabad House), Leopold Cafe and Cama and Albless Hospital.

Terrorism can’t break spirit of nation united in courage: President 

President Droupadi Murmu saluted the courage of brave security personnel and all those who stood fearlessly against the 2008 Mumbai terror attacks and said that “terrorism may shatter lives, but it can never break the spirit of a nation united in courage, compassion, and resilience.”

“Their sacrifice and selfless service will forever remain etched in our nation's soul,” she said in a post on X.

"On this solemn anniversary of the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks, I bow my head in remembrance of the innocent lives lost and salute the courage of our brave soldiers, police personnel, and all those who stood fearlessly against terror," the president said.

“Terrorism may shatter lives, but it can never break the spirit of a nation united in courage, compassion and resilience,” she said.

People walk past a mural condemning the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks on the wall of Rex Bakery near Nariman House in Colaba, on the eve of the 18th anniversary of the attacks, in Mumbai. | Photo Credit: PTI

"Today, we stand with the survivors and their families, reaffirming our collective resolve to eliminate terrorism in all its forms and work towards a more peaceful and humane world," President Murmu said.

Vice-President reaffirms India’s zero-tolerance policy on terrorism

Vice President C P Radhakrishnan aid tribute to the victims of the 2008 Mumbai terror attacks, marking their 18th anniversary, and saluted the courage and supreme sacrifice of security personnel who fought the terrorists.

In a post on X, Mr. Radhakrishnan said he offers heartfelt tributes to the innocent lives lost in the “cowardly terror attacks” and salutes the unparalleled courage and supreme sacrifice of security personnel who fought valiantly to defend the country.

He said India remained unwavering in its commitment to a policy of zero tolerance towards terrorism in all its forms and manifestations and to bringing those responsible to justice.

“The nation stands united in its resolve to defeat terrorism and uphold peace and security,” the vice president said.

Amit Shah lauds India’s role in fighting terrorism

Union Home Minister Amit Shah hailed India’s “leading role” in the global fight against terrorism, acting against its every form, as he paid tribute to the soldiers, police personnel and the innocent people killed in the 2008 Mumbai attacks.

In a post on X on the anniversary of the attack, Mr. Shah said the terrorist attacks on Mumbai on this day in 2008 shook the entire nation.

He saluted the soldiers who laid down their lives while “giving a crushing response” to the terrorists and paid tribute to all the innocent people who lost their lives.

“Terrorism is the most heinous crime against humanity. The Modi government’s ‘zero tolerance’ policy against terror is being praised worldwide. Today, India is playing a leading role in the global fight against terrorism, taking decisive action against every form of terror and every conspiracy,” he said..

Terrorism humanity’s greatest enemy, says Adityanath

Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath said terrorism was humanity’s “greatest enemy” as he paid tributes to those killed in the 2008 Mumbai terror attacks.

In a post on X marking the anniversary of the November 26, 2008 attacks, Mr. Adityanath said, “Humble tributes to all the innocent civilians and brave security personnel who lost their lives in the cowardly terrorist attack in Mumbai on 26/11.” “Terrorism is humanity’s greatest enemy,” he said.

The Chief Minister said a ‘New India’ was committed to eradicating terrorism through unity, determination and a zero-tolerance policy.

India weak when 26/11 attacks occurred : BJP Keralam chief

BJP Keralam president Rajeev Chandrasekhar claimed that India was a weak nation back in 2008 when it answered terror attacks with silence, but now it responds with strength.

Mr. Chandrasekhar made the claim in a post on X while referring to the 26/11 attacks in Mumbai in 2008 that claimed 166 lives.

On the 18th anniversary of the terror attack, the BJP MLA from Nemom assembly constituency paid tributes to the security personnel who lost their lives fighting terrorists.

“18 years since 26/11, when Pakistani terrorists entered India by sea and killed 166 and injured over 300 innocent people in Mumbai.”

“We remember the bravehearts who ran towards the gunfire so that others could live... every jawan, NSG commando, police officer and ordinary citizen, including hotel and railway staff, who stood between Mumbai and terror and gave their lives for it,” he said.

He claimed that “India was a weak, vulnerable nation then”.

“Today’s #NewIndia answers terror with strength, not silence, and we will never let that sacrifice be forgotten,” Chandrasekhar said.

26/11 attack was an assault on Constitution; India will not tolerate terror strikes: Fadnavis

Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis described the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks as an assault on the Constitution, asserting that India would not tolerate attacks on its soldiers or citizens and would strike back against those responsible.

Speaking at a programme at the Gateway of India commemorating the martyrs of the 2008 Mumbai terror attacks, Mr. Fadnavis said the terrorists had deliberately selected locations frequented by foreign nationals and members of the diplomatic community to cause maximum casualties and send a message to the world that India could be attacked.

The attack was not merely on Mumbai but also on the Constitution of India, he said.

Recalling the bravery of police personnel who fought the terrorists, Mr. Fadnavis paid tribute to officers who lost their lives, particularly Tukaram Omble, who captured Pakistani terrorist Ajmal Kasab despite being shot multiple times.

“Kasab had a gun, while Omble had only a lathi. Despite being shot, he managed to capture Kasab,” the Chief Minister said.

He also paid tribute to senior police officers Hemant Karkare, Ashok Kamte and Vijay Salaskar, as well as NSG Major Sandeep Unnikrishnan, who were killed while fighting the terrorists.

Terror strike was a ‘cowardly attack’, says Suvendu Adhikari

West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari paid tribute to those killed in the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks 18 years ago and saluted the security personnel who laid down their lives while protecting the country.

In a post on X, Mr. Adhikari said the terror strike was a “cowardly attack” and asserted that the valour and selfless service of the security personnel would “forever remain etched in the memory of every Indian”.

“On the anniversary of the dreadful 26/11 Mumbai Terror Attack, my heartfelt tributes to all the innocent lives lost in the cowardly attack. May their souls rest in eternal peace,” he said on X.

Mr. Adhikari also said, “I bow in deep gratitude and salute the indomitable courage of our brave security personnel and heroes who made the supreme sacrifice while protecting the sovereignty and integrity of our nation. Their valour and selfless service will forever remain etched in the memory of every Indian.”

India moved a long way since 26/11 Mumbai attacks in security: Assam CM

Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma described November 26, 2008, as a “Black Day” in India’s history, when Pakistani terrorists carried out the Mumbai attacks, killing hundreds of people.

In a social media post, Mr. Sarma said the country had since made significant progress in strengthening its security against both external and internal threats.

“November 26, 2008, will forever be etched in Bharat’s history as a Black Day, when hundreds of innocents were massacred by Pakistani terrorists in the biggest-ever terror strike on the soil of the nation,” he said.

“Since then, we have moved a long way in securing our country from external and internal threats and have vowed to go after every element which is a threat to Bharat’s security,” he added.

The Chief Minister also paid tribute to the victims and the security personnel who laid down their lives during the attacks.

Congress gave clean chit to Pakistan after 26/11 due to vote bank politics, alleges BJP

The BJP on Thursday (November 26, 2026) targeted the Congress and its leader Rahul Gandhi over the 2008 Mumbai terror attacks, accusing it of choosing “vote bank politics” over national security.

In a post on X, BJP national spokesperson Pradeep Bhandari questioned why the Congress-led UPA government did not attack Pakistan after the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks.

“Rahul Gandhi and the Congress Party must answer three questions on the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks: 1. Why did the Congress-led UPA government not attack Pakistan after 26/11 Mumbai terror attack? 2. Why did Congress give a clean chit to Pakistan by calling Mumbai terror Attack “RSS Ki Sajish (conspiracy by RSS)” ? 3. Why was Rahul Gandhi partying just days after 26/11 Mumbai terror attack?”

In his post, Mr. Bhandari also tagged a media report that purportedly spoke about Rahul Gandhi partying soon after the tragic incident.

“Congress gave a clean chit to Pakistan after 26/11 Mumbai terror attack because of vote bank politics of Gandhi-Vadra family! They chose vote bank over nation’s security!” Mr. Bhandari alleged.

Several BJP leaders paid tributes to the victims of the 2008 terror attacks and remembered the security personnel who made the supreme sacrifice while defending the country.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh said, “Today, I pay my humble tributes to the victims of the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks and remember the bravehearts who made the supreme sacrifice in defence of our nation.

“I salute the courage, valour and unwavering dedication of our police and security personnel who stood firm in the face of terror. Their courage reflects the highest traditions of duty, sacrifice and service to the nation. Their supreme sacrifice remains an enduring inspiration. India remains steadfast in its commitment to eliminating terrorism in all its forms,” he said in a post on X.

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar said, “Remember innocent lives lost and salute the brave security personnel who made the supreme sacrifice during 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks. Committed to combating terrorism in all its forms and manifestations and bringing those involved to justice. Zero tolerance for terrorism. #NeverForget.”

BJP national media convenor Ashish Usha Agarwal said, “Heartfelt tributes and deepest respects to the brave soldiers who made the supreme sacrifice defending the nation in the 26/11 Mumbai terror attack, and to the innocent civilians who lost their lives.

“Their courage and sacrifice will forever remain immortal in the hearts of the people of this country. Our unity against terrorism is our greatest strength.”
