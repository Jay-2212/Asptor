Jharkhand BJP delegation meets Governor, seeks Congress MLA’s arrest in assault case

By Amit Bhelari • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-18

The Jharkhand unit of the Bharatiya Janata Party’s Scheduled Tribe Morcha, led by Sunny Toppo, met Governor Santosh Gangwar on Tuesday (November 17, 2026) and submitted a memorandum to him over “rising crimes against women, particularly tribals” in the State.

Highlighting the allegation that Congress’s Simdega MLA Bhushan Bara raped a 14-year-old girl, the Opposition party questioned why no arrest has been made despite the registration of an FIR.

The National Commission for Women and the National Commission for Scheduled Tribes have also sought reports on the issue from the Jharkhand government. The Congress is a constituent of the Jharkhand Mukti Morcha-led ruling coalition.

On November 14, hours after the FIR was registered against Mr. Bara, the police said they had booked two persons, based on a complaint by the girl’s grandfather, for allegedly abducting and coercing the girl into making a “false” statement against the two-time legislator. Leader of the Opposition in the Assembly Babulal Marandi alleged that one of those booked is a journalist.

The Opposition party has sought to corner the Hemant Soren-led government over a series of crimes, which include the Simdega incident, a sexual assault in Hotwar jail and the alleged rape of a woman who had gone to the Gamharia police station seeking justice. Fifteen police officers were suspended in connection with the Gamharia incident on November 1.

The delegation led by Mr. Toppo also called for an impartial probe into the recent death of tribal leader Nisha Kumari Bhagat.

During the day, BJP members staged a demonstration outside Lok Bhavan in Ranchi, demanding the immediate arrest of Mr. Bara. “We are protesting against the sexual exploitation of a 14-year-old tribal girl. The victim has shown great courage by lodging an FIR against the MLA. Instead of properly investigating the matter, action is being taken against a journalist who exposed it,” said senior BJP leader and former MP Geeta Koda.
