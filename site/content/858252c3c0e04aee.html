Consistency, thy name is love?

By Viji Narayan • THE HINDU OPINION • 2026-10-04

As life keeps evolving, one is constantly in search of that emotion called love. In this age of algorithms, AI, and climate change, it is indeed a very tiring task even to talk about love. In a world where romance has almost died a slow death, how does one go love hunting?

“Being deeply loved by someone gives you strength, while loving someone deeply gives you courage.” This quote by Lao Tzu has always remained with me as my mantra for life. Yes, you may not be lucky enough to have found that love which might have given you strength of a lifetime, but you can choose to be courageous enough to love people around you deeply. This indeed asks for a lot of things. Putting the need of that other person if not first, at least as important as yours.

Caring is an extension of loving. The care that one gives parents and children happens because of deep love. In today’s ever-changing world, consistency seems to be a chief ingredient of love. A parent who can quit his job at the drop of the hat because his or her ego clashed with that of a boss or a higher official, cannot claim to love his young children. In the process of saving his ego or clinging to his complexes, he/she conveniently forgets the fact that what they are actually doing is putting their children to hardship, which they definitely should not go through because they came into this world trusting their parents to give them a home and love which they need at every step till they are capable of looking after themselves.

Commitment and consistency are two sides of that coin of love. These are two words many people are afraid of these days. Yes, people love freedom, but commitment and consistency are not antonyms of freedom. Yes, mundane life is boring, no doubt. It is okay to be bored at times. Boring days can sometimes mature into creative days too. A homemaker mother would have wanted to escape the drudgery of cooking and cleaning umpteen times in her life, but she never does that because she has that courage in her to love her children and spouse. She might even have left her dreams but she never gives up on her children. If she happens to be a writer, she has the freedom to write poems and articles about going on a tools-down strike by putting down the ladle and pans or vent about how she wants to abdicate her throne of the empire of taste and aroma. I am definitely not preaching about any superficial sacrifices or remaining in a toxic atmosphere.

My only concern is how commitment is seen as a scary thing these days. How consistency hardly seems to be the in-thing. I did cooking and housekeeping for decades together with unflinching dedication because I had the courage to love the members of my family. Yes, indeed, it needs a lot of courage to love like that. You come across many fathers who work all their lives, not because they have a fancy job or they do not want to quit it. They do it because they want to give a decent living to their loved ones. They have the courage to love too.

Yes, in today’s world, you have the liberty, money and space to experiment with many things in life. Basics of life still remain the same. Your children need food every single day. They need a roof above their heads to protect them from rain and shine. A solid, unhindered, unchanging place to live and be loved which you may choose to call a home if you want. A place where they can come rushing to with their fears at least till they reach their adulthood. If you find this commitment and consistency too smothering, if you want to be a watcher on the shore alone and not dive deep into the sea of life, all I can say is that you indeed lack courage. You need a lot of courage to love too.

In a world of chaos, love alone can bring you some calm. So do not hesitate to love, even if it needs commitment and consistency. Of course, you need some courage: the courage to love.
