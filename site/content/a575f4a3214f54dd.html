    Delhi Police detain around 50 protesters over 'no permission' for Jantar Mantar agitation

    Delhi Congress Chief Devender Yadav, Nehra Bora from All India Students’ Association (AISA) were among those detained from Jantar Mantar over an agitation demanding the removal and arrest of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.

    New Delhi DCP Sachin Sharma said 40-50 protesters were detained as no permission had been sought or granted to hold the protest at the venue. 

    A senior New Delhi police official present at the spot said the protesters were later released. The official denied allegations that police personnel were not wearing name tags, saying all personnel deployed at the spot had been wearing them. 

    The officer also denied allegations of manhandling, saying only the amount of force required to detain the protesters and prevent them from proceeding to the protest site was used.

    Oppn. must fight elections to protect democratic rights: O'Brien on Dipke's boycott call

    Trinamool Congress joint secretary Derek O’Brien on Friday (September 25, 2026) said opposition parties must continue to contest elections to protect democratic rights while simultaneously fighting for free and fair polls.

    He was responding to Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke’s call to boycott elections that he alleged were being rigged.

    “Opposition boycotting elections = BJP wins all seats. Once that happens, they amend the Constitution to legalise all their crimes,” Mr. O’Brien said on X.

    He said staying away from elections would also mean betraying party supporters working on the ground. “Also not contesting elections means betraying the lakhs of workers who are on the ground, fighting with their lives,” he said.

    “That is why it is important to fight elections to protect democratic rights, while also fighting for free and fair elections,” he added.
