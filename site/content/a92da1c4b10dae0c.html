SP declares candidates for five seats in U.P. Assembly polls

By Mayank Kumar • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-25

The Samajwadi Party (SP) on Tuesday (November 24, 2026) announced five candidates for the 2027 Uttar Pradesh Assembly polls.

The party named Ashu Malik as candidate for the Saharanpur assembly seat, Nahid Hasan for Kairana, Mahboob Ali for Amroha , Jyoti Lodhi for Lalitpur and Umar Ali Khan for the Behat assembly seat.

In a related development, SP president Akhilesh Yadav urged Congress to understand public expectations and realities and engage in serious discussions to ensure victory for their alliance in most seats, describing the U.P. assembly election as a historic opportunity for secular parties to defeat the communal BJP and its narrow-minded allies and save the country from falling into the wrong hands and being ruined.

“This time is a historic opportunity when both of us secular parties can together defeat the communal BJP and its narrow-minded allies forever and save the country from falling into wrong hands and getting ruined. We want you and I to understand public expectations and realities and engage in serious discussions to ensure victory for our “alliance” in most seats. In all the seats we contest, our winning numbers should exceed the number of seats we fail to win. Only victory will make the division respectable, because winning seats brings respect,” he wrote on X.

Mr. Yadav asked Congress to mark all potential seats and send them to the SP.

“We will then discuss together which seat and which candidate is in a position to win. Based on our assessment and mathematical equation for victory, we may also inform the Congress about several winnable seats that they, for some reason, haven’t even considered,” he said.

“Our effort is that when big leaders meet, on all the seats, there should be discussion on the candidate of the ‘INDIA Alliance’ and not the candidate of Congress or the SP, and the thing which will give positive results would be that we present our candidate as the ‘PDA candidate’,” he added.

The SP chief claimed ground surveys have made it clear that the message has reached every city, neighborhood, street, and village that aggrieved and humiliated people from every community and every section of society across the state have rallied against the BJP, believing that the PDA will defeat the NDA and bring about change.

“The truth is, we don’t want seat sharing to be based on a “me-or-you” approach. We don’t want it to be a division, we want to call it ‘seat adjustment’. In seats with difficult equations, we will discuss at least two candidates together, choose the one with the most potential, and work together to ensure their victory. We don’t want alliance candidates to be imposed on each other, but rather, they should be decided by consensus,” he added.
