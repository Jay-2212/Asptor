‘Record horticultural production’ shows increase of 4.36% from 2024-25: Agriculture Ministry

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-18

India’s total horticultural production is expected to reach 3,869.15 lakh tonnes in 2025-26, an increase of 161.77 lakh tonnes (4.36%) over the production recorded in 2024-25, according to the third advanced estimates of area and production of horticultural crops released by Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan in New Delhi on Tuesday (November 17, 2026). In the previous crop season, horticulture production stood at 3,707.38 lakh tonnes.

Mr. Chouhan expressed satisfaction over the projected growth and said the significant increase in horticultural production reflected the hard work of India’s farmers and the impact of the Union government’s farmer-friendly policies. “This record production would not only strengthen the country’s nutritional security but also directly contribute to higher incomes for horticulture farmers,” the Minister said, congratulating farmers across the country for their efforts.

Farmers were increasingly moving beyond traditional crops towards the cultivation of fruits, vegetables, flowers, spices, and plantation crops, including cashew, coconut and cocoa, as well as aromatic and medicinal plants, he said.

“This growing interest in commercial and high-value crops is opening up new avenues of income for farmers while creating fresh opportunities for self-reliance and growth in the agricultural sector,” Mr. Chouhan said, adding that one of the most significant aspects of the latest estimates was increase in production despite a reduction in the area under horticultural cultivation.

“The total area under horticultural crops in 2025-26 is estimated at approximately 297.21 lakh hectares, compared with 301.36 lakh hectares in 2024-25. Despite the decline in cultivated area, production is projected to increase across fruits, vegetables, flowers, plantation crops, and aromatic and medicinal plants,” Mr. Chouhan said.

Higher fruit production

Fruit production is estimated to rise from 1,176.49 lakh tonnes in 2024-25 to 1,230.66 lakh tonnes in 2025-26, an increase of 54.18 lakh tonnes or 4.60%. “The improved output is primarily attributed to better production of banana, mango, papaya, apple, pomegranate and jackfruit,” the Union Agriculture Ministry said in a release.

The area under vegetables is estimated to increase from 117.91 lakh hectares to 119.31 lakh hectares. “Vegetable production is projected to rise by 4.35%, from 2,177.97 lakh tonnes in 2024-25 to 2,272.68 lakh tonnes in 2025-26. The increase is primarily driven by higher production of potato, tomato, brinjal, bottle gourd, cucumber, cabbage, beans and peas,” the release added. Potato and tomato production are both expected to register strong growth. Potato production is estimated at 655.70 lakh tonnes, up 11.95% from 585.71 lakh tonnes in the previous year. Tomato production is projected to increase from 205.99 lakh tonnes to 214.11 lakh tonnes. Onion production is estimated at 288.96 lakh tonnes.

Floriculture growth

Flower production is projected to rise by approximately 14.45%, from 42.65 lakh tonnes in 2024-25 to 48.82 lakh tonnes in 2025-26. “The area under flower cultivation has also increased to 4.10 lakh hectares,” it added. In the production of plantation crops, including arecanut, coconut, cashew, betel leaves and cocoa. “Their combined production is estimated to increase by 7.56% to 182.64 lakh tonnes. Production of aromatic and medicinal plants is also expected to rise from 9.01 lakh tonnes to 9.28 lakh tonnes,” the Ministry of Agriculture added.

The production of turmeric, garlic, curry leaves and cardamom is also expected to grow. “The total area under spices in 2025-26 is estimated at 47.56 lakh hectares, with production projected at 123.54 lakh tonnes,” the Ministry said.
