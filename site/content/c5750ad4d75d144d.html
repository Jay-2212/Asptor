Rahul Gandhi dares PM to debate him on who is ‘anti-national’

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-10

A day after Prime Minister Narendra Modi cautioned Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) personnel to be alert about “dimagi [intellectual] Naxals”, the Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi on Monday (November 9, 2026) dared the Prime Minister to debate him on who was an anti-national.

In a post on social media platform X addressed to CRPF personnel, Mr. Gandhi said their expressions showed they had not been convinced by the Prime Minister’s remarks. “Your faces said it all: you didn’t buy the PM’s lies,” he said.

Accusing Mr. Modi of branding his political opponents as “Naxals”, Mr. Gandhi reiterated his challenge to debate him on “who is anti-national and who is controlled by foreign forces”.

“He called us Naxals. My challenge stands. Let him debate me on who is anti-national and who is controlled by foreign forces. Any time, any place. My guarantee: after the debate, the PM won’t have the guts to stand in front of a single jawan again,” the Congress leader said.

On Sunday (November 8, 2026), addressing security personnel at a CRPF camp in Bastar, Chhattisgarh, during his Deepavali visit, Mr. Modi said that while armed Maoists had been eliminated, “dimagi Naxals” had infiltrated political parties and organisations, and were instigating people against the Constitution and democracy. He called for identifying and defeating such elements.

The Prime Minister had also used the term in his Independence Day address in August, arguing that ideological supporters of Naxalism continued to pose a challenge despite success in eliminating armed insurgency.

The Congress accused Mr. Modi of being “distraught, disturbed and desperate”, adding that his “jhola (bag)-picking-up time” was approaching.

“The Prime Minister has not spared the country his usual abusive language even on Diwali. Once again, he has called some of his political opponents ‘intellectual Naxals’. Most objectionable is that he abused an extremely solemn and non-political occasion for this, wore the uniform of the security forces, and hurled a barrage of mud at his political opponents. This is an open assault on all norms of propriety and tradition. But when have these gentlemen ever cared about principles and ideals?” Congress’s communications chief Jairam Ramesh said in a post on X on Sunday.

Mr. Gandhi has repeatedly challenged Mr. Modi to an open debate, claiming that he could establish within five minutes that the Prime Minister was a “traitor”. Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) leaders have dismissed the challenge, accusing the Congress leader of avoiding debates in Parliament.

Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman has said that Parliament was already an available forum for debate and asked Mr. Gandhi to show “some seriousness and responsibility” in discharging his role as the Leader of the Opposition.
