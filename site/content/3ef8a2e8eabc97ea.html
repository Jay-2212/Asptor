Revisit SIR exercise, CEC must resign: Naveen Patnaik on report of internal disquiet in poll panel

By Satyasundar Barik • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-09-26

Biju Janata Dal President and former Odisha Chief Minister Naveen Patnaik on Friday (September 25, 2026) demanded the resignation of the Chief Election Commissioner following a media expose that pointed out differences in opinion about Special Intensive Revision-2026.

Election Commission row LIVE: September 25, 2026

“Because of strong and sharp differences between various election commissioners, the CEC should immediately resign. And the entire process of SIR should be thoroughly revisited,” Mr. Patnaik said here.

The BJD has been against the SIR process from the very beginning, he said. The BJD had alleged that names of a large number of eligible voters had been excluded from the final electoral roll under SIR in Odisha.

“The SIR 2026 process was initiated by mapping the 2025 electoral data. Instead of simplifying the process, it was deliberately made complicated. As a result, voters were excluded from the list of eligible voters,” the BJD said.

“According to the 2025 electoral roll, at the time of notification of the SIR process on May 14, 2026, the electoral roll available on the website contained 3,40,72,744 voters in the State. The same had also been mapped with the 2002 SIR. At the beginning of the SIR process, the Chief Electoral Officer of the State had stated that 3.16 crore voters had been mapped. The BJD had questioned where the remaining voters had vanished,” the party said in a statement.

The BJD maintained, “Thereafter, during the form-filling process, the number of voters stood at 3,33,93,531. However, as proper awareness was not created among voters during the form-filling process, people faced difficulties in submitting their applications.”

“The office of the Chief Electoral Officer had stated in the draft electoral roll that there were 3,13,87,034 voters. Comparing this figure with the initial data, the BJD had stated that approximately 27 lakh voters had been excluded from the electoral roll. However, the Election Officer stated that 20,12,000 voters had been excluded from the electoral roll. There was, however, no consistency between the statement and the data provided,” the party further said.

Mr. Patnaik again raised the issue of Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Act 2026.

“As far as MMDR Act is concerned why blame others when 20 Lok Sabha MPs from Bharatiya Janata Party supported this in the Parliament. We will in BJD take this strongly up with the State government to see to this it is revised thoroughly. In this process, it is too much of loss of revenue for the State.”
