Independent SIR Commission asks CM Adityanath for proof of his statement that 1.27 crore Hindus excluded

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-21

The Independent Commission on the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls, a civil society initiative, wrote to Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath on Saturday (November 21), requesting him to share supporting material or data relating to his reported public statement that about 1.27 crore Hindus from the State have been excluded from the voters’ list.

The Commission, which was initiated by a body of Supreme Court and High Court lawyers called the Lawyers Association for Constitution (LAFC) to respond to widespread concerns about the SIR, is chaired by former apex court judge, Justice Madan B. Lokur.

The members of the Commission include former Supreme Court judge, Justice A.K. Patnaik; Justice Badar Durrez Ahmad, former Chief Justice of the Jammu and Kashmir High Court; Justice Rekha Sharma, former judge of the Delhi High Court; and Justice Anjana Prakash, former judge of the Patna High Court.

In its letter to the Chief Minister, the Commission said his statement was directly relevant to its mandate of examining whether eligible electors were unfairly removed from the rolls during the SIR exercise.

The five-member Commission is independently evaluating the “constitutional and statutory validity, methodology, and overall impact of the SIR exercise on eligible voters”.

“Your public statement, which was widely reported in the media, to the effect that 1.27 crore Hindus from Uttar Pradesh have been excluded from the voters’ list is particularly relevant to the mandate. The Commission requests you to kindly furnish any supporting material, or any substantive data, or anything that may indicate the gaps in methodology, which may have resulted in such alleged deletions. We will examine the material with complete autonomy and impartiality to arrive at our final findings,” the Commission’s letter said.

It urged the Chief Minister not to delay his response owing to the urgency of the issue because of impending elections in Uttar Pradesh, and the possible denial of voting rights of millions of electors.

“The Commission is working with a tight timeline and would appreciate it if the necessary material is provided at the earliest,” the Commission said, providing its email to Chief Minister Adityanath.
