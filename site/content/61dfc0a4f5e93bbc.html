BJP reshuffle: Ram Madhav, Smriti Irani, Biplab Deb in new national team; Piyush Goyal named treasurer

By Nistula Hebbar • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-08-17

Seven months after he took charge as the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) president, Nitin Nabin on Monday (August 17, 2026) unveiled his national organisational team. Although peppered with some new faces, the list signals continuity and organisational experience in major positions; the return of some important party men and women; and a clear imprint of recent events, including the Gen Z protests on the appointments related to the party’s social media communications.

Inclusions, exclusions

Former Union Minister Smriti Irani as general secretary, and former party general secretary (during the presidentship of Union Minister Amit Shah) Ram Madhav as vice president have made a comeback to the BJP’s organisation.

The inclusion of former Tripura Chief Minister Biplab Deb and Rajya Sabha MP Satish Poonia as general secretaries signals that they have been rewarded for their organisational work during the West Bengal and Haryana Assembly elections, respectively.

Only two general secretaries in the previous team have been retained — Sunil Bansal and Vinod Tawde. The new general secretaries include, apart from Ms. Irani, Mr. Poonia and Mr. Deb, former Lok Sabha MP from Uttar Pradesh Harish Dwivedi, current Rajya Sabha MP Sanjay Bhatia (who resigned his Lok Sabha seat of Karnal in 2024 to make way for Union Minister Manohar Lal Khattar to contest from it), and Gajendra Patel from Madhya Pradesh.

The appointment with the most sharp signalling, however, appears to be in the BJP’s social media team. After a long stint at the helm, Amit Malviya will no longer be in charge. Deepak Mhaskey from Chhattisgarh replaces him.

The appointment of Union Minister of Commerce Piyush Goyal as treasurer (a post he held from 2010-14), thus holding two posts, one in the government and another in the party organisation, is also significant.

Mr. Mhaskey managed the social media team for the BJP in Chhattisgarh when Mr. Nabin was the State in-charge during the 2023 Assembly election, which the party won. The appointment, and a new team made up of Priti Gandhi, Alok Bhatt, Shiwanand Dwivedi, and Arun Yadav is expected to provide a relook to the BJP’s online strategy after the lacerations to the party’s image during the Gen Z protests in July.

Although considered a relatively young appointee to the national president’s position, Mr. Nabin has clearly managed to leave his imprint on the team, with several inductions of those enjoying his trust from before his appointment as president.

The BJP’s media cell, meanwhile, sees both continuity and change, with Anil Baluni, MP from Uttarakhand, retained as the chief of the media cell, assisted by Pradeep Bhandari, Ashish Agarwal, and Siddharth Yadav.

The BJP’s expansion, and the splitting of other parties with people joining from there, have also been kept in mind. Sandeep Pathak, formerly of the Aam Aadmi Party, and Rohan Gupta, who joined from the Congress, are now BJP national secretaries. Some of the new faces among the national secretaries include Swadesh Singh, Siddharth Shambhu, and Rajya Sabha MP from Nagaland Phangnon Konyak.

The all important position of general secretary (organisation) also sees continuity with the retention of B.L. Santosh, along with Shiv Prakash and Saudan Singh. All morcha or cell chiefs have been changed, with Lok Sabha MP from Madhya Pradesh and former chief of the State V.D. Sharma made the coordinator for all the cells, including the youth wing, the Mahila Morcha, and the cells for Scheduled Castes, Schedule Tribes, and the Other Backward Classes.

The immediate challenge for the new team will be the upcoming Assembly elections in Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Punjab, and Goa, explaining the presence of Mr. Tawde, Mr. Ram Madhav, Mr. Poonia, and Mr. Harish Dwivedi.

Youth and experience

Amid talk that the new team should have a generational shift, the continuity in roles by incumbents signals that Mr. Nabin is proceeding with caution. Of the total of 64 office-bearers, six members of the new team are below 40 years of age, 15 members between the ages of 40-49 years, and 21 members between 50-59 years. There are 12 women among the office-bearers, six of them vice presidents, and around 40% of the team comes from marginalised communities across 23 States.

The new team announced by Mr. Nabin has tried to strike a balance between the exigencies of immediate political challenges, and the fact that this would also be the team under which the BJP will face the 2029 Lok Sabha election.

PM, ministers congratulate newly appointed BJP office-bearers

Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated the newly appointed national office-bearers of the BJP, saying the team brings together organisational experience, energy and grassroots connect.

“Congratulations to the newly appointed national office bearers of the BJP and those who have been given various responsibilities in the party. This newly appointed team brings together organisational experience, energy and grassroots connect,” PM Modi said on X.

The Prime Minister said he was confident the new team will give their best in strengthening the BJP and focusing on public service. “My best wishes to them,” he said.
