BJP slams Kharge for calling Diwali 'Eid of joy', dares Congress to call Eid 'Diwali of joy'

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-10

The BJP on Monday (November 9, 2026) came down heavily on Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge of insulting Hindus by describing Deepavali as “Eid of happiness”, alleging that the statement reflected the principal Opposition party’s “politics of appeasement” and “anti-Sanatan” ideology.

“Whatever statements Mallikarjun Kharge ji makes should be understood as the anti-Hindu and anti-Sanatan thinking of Rahul Gandhi and Sonia Gandhi. Mallikarjun Kharge ji is advancing that very same thinking,” BJP spokesperson Gaurav Bhatia said at a press conference.

BJP’s challenge

“I issue a challenge to Rahul Gandhi and Kharge ji. When Eid comes this time, Kharge ji and Rahul Gandhi ji, please make sure you say this once: ‘The Diwali of happiness has arrived’... This will demonstrate whether your thinking and ideology are consistent. If Diwali is an ‘Eid of happiness’, then Eid should also be a ‘Diwali of happiness’ for Kharge ji. However, this will not happen, as the remarks are a reflection of the Congress’s mindset,” Mr. Bhatia said.

Congress leader Pawan Khera defended Mr. Kharge’s “Khushiyon ka Eid (Eid of happiness)” remark, saying those criticising it were “semantically ignorant” as the word ‘Eid’ literally meant festival. He said Mr. Kharge’s statement was perfectly normal and accused the BJP of viewing even a festival through the lens of negativity. “We come from the Ganga-Jamuna tehzeeb [syncretic culture]. We will bring all religions along together,” Mr. Khera added.

Earlier, describing Deepavali as one of India’s biggest festivals that taught harmony, Mr. Bhatia asked: “Why is it that when we are talking about Diwali, which has its own sanctity, its own tradition and identity, you are congratulating Hindus by calling it Eid? Has the character and DNA of the Congress party now become such that Hindu festivals should have their identities changed?”

‘Appeasement’

“They first indulge in blatant appeasement of the Muslim community and, even during a festival that represents joy, happiness and inclusiveness, hurt the sentiments of the Hindu community,” Mr. Bhatia said. He also criticised Congress leader Rashid Alvi for defending Mr. Kharge’s statement, citing Mr. Alvi’s argument that Eid was often used as a metaphor to express joy.

Referring to his remarks that “there is a distinction between Eid and Diwali: while both are festivals of joy, Eid does not lead to an increase in the AQI (Air Quality Index)”, Mr. Bhatia alleged that Congress leaders did not raise similar concerns about Bakrid. “When our Muslim brothers and sisters celebrate Bakrid, they sacrifice goats. Blood flows and enters the water, causing water pollution,” he said.
