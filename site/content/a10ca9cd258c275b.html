After daylong operation against terrorists in Kashmir’s Kulgam, searches on to trace the bodies

By Peerzada Ashiq • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-17

A group of terrorists, believed to be responsible for the November 11 terror attack, was encircled by security forces in south Kashmir’s Kulgam on Monday, following six days of anti-terror operations in parts of the district

A gunfight broke out between the hiding terrorists and the security forces in the Laroo Bypass area, which lies about 16 km from the site of last week’s attack, in which terrorists opened fire on a Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) checkpost, injuring three personnel.

Searches were ongoing till late on Monday evening (November 16, 2026) to trace the bodies of terrorists believed to have been killed in the gunfight, and to track any injured terrorists present in the area, which has both residential houses as well as orchards in its vicinity. “A combing operation is on,” officials said.

“The whole area has been cordoned off. The J&K Police and the security forces are on the job,” a police spokesman said.

Reports of one dead terrorist

J&K Director General of Police Nalin Prabhat and Inspector General of Police Sujit Kumar were spearheading the operation, along with the CRPF and the Army.

Unconfirmed reports suggested one terrorist carrying a rifle was spotted dead at the site. However, the police were tight-lipped about the details of the ongoing operation and have not officially confirmed retrieving any bodies from the area.

Initial reports suggested that Lashkar-e-Taiba-affiliated local terrorist Mohammad Lateef Bhat, 22, and his two accomplices, who were behind the November 11 attack, could be trapped at the encounter site. The J&K Police has been tracking Bhat’s movements since his alleged involvement in the twin attacks in Anantnag and Kulgam in July this year, which left a policeman and two migrant workers dead.

Earlier in the day, CRPF Special Director General Sandeep Khirwar said the security forces would “continue operations”.

“The situation is under control. We made some headway, we’ve got some leads which we are hopeful we will be able to see through to the culmination, to the logical end. And our jawans, all the forces that we have, we have told them all to maintain their operational discipline, follow [standard operating procedures] SOPs, and maintain alertness,” said Mr. Khirwar.

‘Kashmiris deserve security without fear’

Commenting on the prolonged operation in Kulgam, Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) leader Sartaj Madani said, “The situation is increasingly reminiscent of the difficult days of the 1990s. We cannot allow the people of Kashmir to be pushed back into an era they have suffered immensely to overcome. Let me make it absolutely clear: security must never be compromised, but neither can human rights, dignity and the fundamental freedoms of common citizens be compromised in the name of security.”

The Opposition leader’s statement came hours before the gunfight broke out in the area. Hitting out at the ruling National Conference (NC), Mr. Madani said, “Equally disappointing is the deafening silence of the elected government. People voted for a popular government with the hope that their voices would be heard, their rights protected and their concerns addressed. Today, when people are facing restrictions and uncertainty, where is that government? Where are its representatives? Not even a meaningful statement of concern has come from those who sought the people’s mandate.”

He said the people of Kashmir have endured enough. “They deserve security without fear, governance without silence, and dignity without conditions. A popular mandate is a responsibility to stand with the people, not a licence to remain silent when they suffer,” the PDP leader added.
