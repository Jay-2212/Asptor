Owaisi names Chandrashekhar Azad as Uttar Pradesh CM face of Samajik Parivartan Morcha

By Mayank Kumar • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-12

All India Majlis-e-Ittehadul Muslimeen (AIMIM) president Asaduddin Owaisi and Azad Samaj Party (Kanshi Ram) chief Chandrashekhar Azad on Thursday announced a new political alliance, the Samajik Parivartan Morcha, for the 2027 Uttar Pradesh Assembly elections.

This is the first declared non-NDA, non-INDIA political front in U.P., with both parties considered minor players eyeing the consolidation of marginalised voters in the State.

‘Azad our CM candidate’

“We have been holding discussions for quite some time. Under the alliance framework, we have declared Chandrashekhar Azad as our chief ministerial candidate. Our aim is to address the issues faced by the people of Uttar Pradesh. The BJP has ruined U.P.’s education system. The future of the State’s youth is being destroyed, and unemployment has become the norm. Other political parties failed to stop the BJP in Uttar Pradesh, as witnessed in the elections of 2014, 2017, 2019, 2022, and 2024. Our alliance will emerge as a strong alternative. We are hopeful and will work hard to achieve our political objectives,” said Mr. Owaisi, the Hyderabad Lok Sabha MP.

‘Front for social change’

“This is not a third front, but a front for social change. Our alliance is strong on the ground, and it will grow even stronger. It is an alliance of deprived, downtrodden, marginalised, oppressed, and exploited people and groups. Our promise is to eradicate poverty and helplessness, and to put an end to the caste-based violence and oppression inflicted upon us in Uttar Pradesh,” said Mr. Azad, Lok Sabha MP from Nagina.

He said that those who spoke of uplifting poor, marginalised social groups and bringing them into the mainstream went on to become MLAs, MPs, Chief Ministers, and even Prime Ministers. “Yet, these marginalised communities remain exactly where they have always been. The truth is that governments kept changing, announcements kept being made, and even today, the Bahujan community remains deprived of basic amenities; in Uttar Pradesh, these communities face injustice due to bulldozer Raj,” he added.

The Nagina MP was also asked if he would join hands with the Samajwadi Party (SP) and the Congress. “Our heart is not small. The main opposition alliance failed to defeat the BJP in previous elections. I urge other parties to join our coalition to defeat the BJP. However, there is a lot of arrogance; still, we remain open to a united front against the BJP,” said Mr. Azad.

Mr. Azad and Mr. Owaisi arrived separately in Lucknow for the joint press conference.

SP welcomes new front

Meanwhile, SP president Akhilesh Yadav welcomed the new political front.

“We welcome the ‘Social Change Front’. Change must occur at the societal level, but true success will only be achieved when the “Political Change Front” is activated. We will support anyone who truly fights against communal forces for the betterment of society and raises the voice of the PDA community, in making their honest voice reach the public. One benefit of this for the opposition is that those who used to vote for communal forces after being swayed by emotional rhetoric and deception will no longer turn to them. These naive people who have been misled and voted will now have another option. This will result in a further loss of 2-3% votes for the BJP. The BJP will be left with nothing. The public understands the truth of those whose sole aim is to generate excitement through words and statements. The truth is that such protests strengthen democracy, provided they are motivated by the welfare of one’s own community, not by the ulterior motives of a third party. If someone becomes a puppet of politics, the public can even have their bail forfeited. Hopefully, the practice of “vote-contracting” in politics will end, and everyone will work for the true benefit of their community,” wrote Mr. Yadav on X.

Focus on marginalised communities

The Dalit and minority communities account for over 40% of the State’s total electorate.

“The alliance will aim to focus on Dalit and minority voters, as both leaders have long been associated with these vote banks. But the larger issue will be securing votes. Winning one’s own seat is one matter, but ensuring your candidates win their seats is a completely different matter. In recent times, U.P. politics has increasingly transformed into a bipolar contest between the NDA and the INDIA alliance. It will be a huge challenge for this alliance to make an impact, but with the Nagina MP as the CM candidate, the new alliance offers an alternative to a large population. It may force the INDIA alliance to revisit its strategy on many seats and adjust its agenda going into the polls,” said Asad Rizvi, a political analyst based in Lucknow.

In the 2022 U.P. assembly polls, AIMIM formed an alliance with the lesser-known Jan Adhikar Party led by Babu Singh Kushwaha, the Bharat Mukti Morcha led by Waman Meshram, the Janata Kranti Party led by Anil Singh Chauhan, and the Bharatiya Vanchit Samaj Party led by Ram Prasad Kashyap. However, it did not win any seats despite contesting in 95 constituencies. Similarly, the Azad Samaj Party (Kanshi Ram), led by Mr. Azad, contested 110 seats but could not secure a seat.

“The new alliance addresses the injustice faced by marginalised sections and vows to fight for their rights. It remains to be seen how it will make an impact amid an increasingly bipolar contest in U.P. Last time, both parties contested the polls but failed to make an impact,” said Umakant Pandey, a political commentator.
