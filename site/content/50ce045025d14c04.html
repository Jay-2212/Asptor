    Odisha deploys 74 rescue teams, starts evacuation as Cyclone set to hit neighbouring Andhra

    The Odisha government yesterday geared up for the possible impact of Cyclone Akvan, the third cyclonic storm formed over the Bay of Bengal in three weeks, with the deployment of 74 rescue teams and early evacuation of vulnerable people in the southern districts.

    Disaster Management Minister Suresh Pujari held a meeting to review preparedness based on the latest IMD forecast and said the government made “full arrangements to secure lives and property”.

    According to the latest bulletin issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD), a deep depression over the southeast and adjoining southwest Bay of Bengal moved with a speed of 10 kmph during the past six hours till 5.30 pm, and intensified into a cyclonic storm Akvan.

    It lay centred at a distance of 790 km south of Gopalpur in Odisha.

    It is very likely to turn into a very severe cyclonic storm around November 19 morning and cross Andhra Pradesh between Nellore and Tuni, around noon the next day, with the maximum sustained wind speed of 120-130 kmph gusting to 145 kmph, the IMD said.
