Higher pension to 4.45 lakh applicants may cost EPFO ₹1.1 lakh crore

By A. M. Jigeesh • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-19

Four years after a Supreme Court judgement on provident fund pension based on higher wages, the Employees Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has said that it may have to spend an additional ₹1.109 lakh crore to implement the order and pay higher pensions to about 4.45 lakh applicants. In a communication to the Central Board of Trustees, which is meeting here on Thursday, the EPFO said it considered 15.24 lakh applications for higher pension and, as on November 13, demand letters were issued to 4,45,471 applicants and pension payment orders (PPOs) have been issued to 1,51,874 pensioners.

The EPFO received 17.49 lakh applications for pension on higher wages after the Supreme Court judgment, of which 2.24 lakh applications were not forwarded by the employers till the last date of January 31, 2025. Out of around 15.24 lakh applications received by the EPFO, 99.2 per cent have been disposed of and 4,45,471 demand letters have been issued. Out of these, 80,067 cases have been found ineligible later due to non-remittance of demand amount. “Around 2,62,102 applicants have deposited the demand amount/ consent out of which 1,03,793 are continuing in service and 1,58,309 have already retired. Out of these applicants, 1,53,901 have submitted Form-10D or are already pensioners. PPOs have been issued to a total of around 1,51,874 applicants. In addition, 2,027 PPOs are under finalisation and will be issued very shortly,” the EPFO said in the communication to its trustees.

The total additional liability is ₹78,501.42 crore for 3,15,601 applications. “The per-person liability is estimated at ₹25.99 lakh for active members and ₹23.14 lakh for pensioners, resulting in an overall average per-person liability of around ₹24.9 lakh across both groups,” the EPFO said.

It added that till November 13, demand amount or consent has been received from 2,62,102 applicants to the joint option for which the additional proportionate liability would be approximately ₹65,263 crore. “Similarly, the estimated additional proportionate liability considering approval of 100 per cent of total applications for which demand notices have been issued (i.e., 4.454 lakh) would be about ₹1.109 lakh crore. This estimation, however, is likely to vary in case any additional set of employees, members, pensioners become eligible for any reason whatsoever, in future, particularly on account of the ongoing litigations,” the EPFO said.

To resolve the delays, the EPFO has said timely Issue of PPOs and release of arrears will be implemented and all legal cases will be defended properly to ensure that there is no adverse legal consequence. It has urged the CBT to take note of the information and give directions to the EPFO on the matter.
