    Cyclone Akvan veers towards North Andhra, Odisha; IMD issues red alert for two districts

    “Severe cyclonic storm Akvan is likely to cross the North Andhra Pradesh, Yanam and South Odisha coasts between Narsapur and Puri, close to Gopalpur, by noon on Friday (November 20, 2026),” the IMD said.

    After moving northwards, the system lay over the west-central Bay of Bengal at 2.30 p.m. on Thursday, about 390 km east-southeast of Narsapur, 320 km south-southeast of Kalingapatnam, 410 km south of Gopalpur, 470 km south of Puri and 610 km east-northeast of Chennai, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said in a press release.

    “It (Akvan) is likely to move north-northwestward and cross the North Andhra Pradesh, Yanam and South Odisha coasts between Narsapur and Puri, close to Gopalpur, by noon on November 20, 2026, with maximum sustained wind speeds of 90-100 kmph (and) gusting to 110 kmph,” it said.

    The system was earlier expected to cross the coast along mid-coastal Andhra Pradesh but subsequently changed course and veered towards North Andhra Pradesh and Odisha.

    In view of the cyclone, the IMD issued a red alert for Srikakulam and Vizianagaram districts on Thursday, an orange alert for Parvatipuram Manyam, Alluri Sitaramaraju (ASR) and Anakapalli districts, and a yellow alert for Kakinada.

    “It forecasts extremely heavy rainfall at isolated places in Srikakulam and Vizianagaram, and heavy to very heavy rainfall in the orange alert districts. Heavy rainfall is likely at isolated places in Kakinada district,” it said. - PTI

    South Eastern Railway cancels several trains as Cyclone Akvan approaches Odisha coast

    The South Eastern Railway announced the cancellation of at least 28 express trains, most to Puri or passing through the temple town, for Friday (November 20, 2026) as a precautionary response to severe cyclonic storm Akvan approaching the Odisha coast.

    “The cyclone is set to hit the coast between Narsapur in Andhra Pradesh and Puri in Odisha on Friday. The Shalimar-Puri Express and Bhanjpur-Puri Express, scheduled to depart on Thursday, were cancelled,” a South Eastern Railway official said.

    He said the train cancellations on Thursday and Friday were for West Bengal, Odisha and Jharkhand, owing to the weather conditions.

    “The Howrah-Puri-Howrah Vande Bharat Express have been cancelled on Friday,” the official said. The other trains cancelled on Friday include Puri-Bhanjpur Express, Bangriposi-Puri-Bangriposi Express and the Brahmapur-Tatanagar-Brahmapur Express. Several other passenger trains were also cancelled in the zone across Odisha and neighbouring Jharkhand. - PTI

    ECoR cancels 39 trains, reschedules 28 others

    “The East Coast Railway (ECoR) cancelled 39 trains scheduled to operate on November 19, 20 and 21 as a precautionary measure in view of Cyclone Akvan,” an official said.

    The cancellations cover several long-distance express trains, Vande Bharat, passenger and MEMU services connecting Odisha with neighbouring States. Several trains operating to and from Puri, Bhubaneswar, Cuttack and Brahmapur have also been affected. “Besides, 28 other trains have been rescheduled, with changes in their departure timings,” the official said.

    Among the cancelled services are the Bhubaneswar-Visakhapatnam Vande Bharat Express (20841/20842), Howrah-Puri Vande Bharat Express (22895/22896), Tatanagar-Brahmapur Vande Bharat Express (20891/20892) and Puri-Yesvantpur Garib Rath Express (22883/22884). The ECoR advised passengers to take note of the cancellation of these train services and plan their journeys accordingly.

    Passengers have also been advised to check the latest train timings and other details through the National Train Enquiry System (NTES) or other official railway enquiry channels before commencing their journey. - PTI

    Cyclone 'Akvan': IMD forecasts heavy rain in south Bengal till Nov. 21

    The IMD on Thursday (November 19, 2026) forecast heavy rain in the southern districts of West Bengal in the next two days as cyclonic storm ‘Akvan’ approaches the east coast.

    Cyclone ‘Akvan’ heads for Odisha; over one lakh people evacuated

    Odisha on Thursday (November 19, 2026) stepped up evacuation of people, positioning of disaster response forces and emptying of reservoirs in southern and coastal districts as the Severe Cyclonic Storm ‘Akvan’ is expected cross the State’s coast, close to east of Gopalpur, by noon of November 20.

    Cyclone Akvan: 80 Andhra fishermen stranded at Odisha jetty

    Around 80 fishermen from Andhra Pradesh’s Kakinada, along with 10 motorised boats, have taken shelter in Odisha’s Ganjam district as rough sea conditions under the influence of severe cyclonic storm Akvan prevented them from returning home, officials said on Thursday (November 19, 2026).

    What weather experts say on the revised landfall point of cyclone Akvan

    After a day of uncertainty that kept cyclone forecasters puzzled, the new projected track of ​severe cyclonic storm Akvan​ points to a probable landfall on the ​Odisha​ coast, near Gopalpur.

    However, weather experts at the India Meteorological Department (IMD), Amaravati, feel there is a slight possibility of the system changing its track and making landfall near the extreme end of north coastal Andhra Pradesh. The IMD continues to say that the landfall corridor stretches from Narsapur in Andhra Pradesh to Puri in Odisha. The impact of the cyclone on ​Andhra Pradesh​ has lessened, but vigil is being maintained, since north coastal Andhra Pradesh has been put on an extremely heavy rainfall alert.

    The system was initially thought to cross the central Andhra Pradesh coast, near Bapatla and Krishna districts. But its trajectory has changed constantly since cyclogenesis, said P.L.N. Murthy, Head of the Cyclone Warning Centre, Visakhapatnam.
