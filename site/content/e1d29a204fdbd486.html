Prime Minister Narendra Modi is visiting Manila on November 16-17 to participate in the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit at the invitation of Philippine President Ferdinand R Marcos Jr.

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-16

Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Monday (November 16, 2026) embarked on a visit to Manila in the Philippines to participate in the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit on November 17, 2026. Mr. Modi is attending the summits at the invitation of President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.

Ahead of his departure, Mr. Modi, in a statement, said, “Our relationship with ASEAN countries is at the heart of India’s Act East Policy and occupies a central place in our MAHASAGAR vision. Our ties, rooted in centuries of civilizational exchanges, have grown into a dynamic partnership spanning trade, connectivity, maritime security, technology and people-to-people relations.”

“At the ASEAN-India Summit, I look forward to working with fellow leaders to build on the progress of our Comprehensive Strategic Partnership and give further momentum to our shared aspirations for growth, connectivity and prosperity,” he added.

Talking about The East Asia Summit, Mr. Modi said that it ‘provides an important platform for dialogue on regional and global challenges’.

“I look forward to exchanging views with participating leaders on strengthening peace, stability and cooperation in the Indo-Pacific,” the Prime Minister said.

Mr. Modi also said he looks forward to interacting with the vibrant Indian community in the Philippines ‘whose contributions strengthen the bonds of friendship between our two countries’.

“I am confident that the visit will impart new energy to India’s partnership with ASEAN and reinforce our collective commitment to a peaceful, prosperous and resilient Indo-Pacific,” he added.

Quad leaders to meet on November 17

A meeting of the leaders of the Quad group — comprising the U.S., India, Australia and Japan — is scheduled to be held in Manila on the sidelines of the ASEAN Summit, a senior U.S. official said.

The meeting of the Quad leaders, the first in two years, will bring together Prime Minister Narendra Modi, U.S. President Donald Trump, Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese, and Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi.

The last meeting of Quad leaders was chaired by then U.S. President Joe Biden in Delaware on September 21, 2024.

“On Tuesday (November 17, 2026) evening in Manila… the President will participate in a multilateral meeting with Quad leaders to discuss shared interests of peace, prosperity, and security in the Indo-Pacific region,” Olivia Wales, Assistant Press Secretary at the White House, said.

She said under President Trump’s leadership, the Quad has demonstrated that it is a partnership of action, tackling the region’s toughest challenges and driving opportunities to ensure a free and open Indo-Pacific.

The Quad group was created in 2007, but went mostly dormant the year after amid concerns about provoking China.

In 2017, Mr. Trump revived it to address growing concerns about China’s increasing economic, diplomatic and military heft in the Indo-Pacific, though he never held a leaders’ summit.

The Manila meeting will be the Quad leaders’ first meeting during Mr. Trump’s present term as U.S. President.
