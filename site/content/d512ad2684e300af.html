    CJI says lawyers must not masquerade political agendas as professional duty

    Chief Justice of India Surya Kant said political agendas of lawyers must not be masqueraded as professional duties, nor should judicial platforms be targeted for personal gains.

    “Disagreement is not a weakness of constitutional democracy. A strong Bar must be able to question, challenge and disagree. Equally, a confident institution must have the capacity to engage with criticism without treating every disagreement as a challenge to its authority. What matters is that such engagement must remain guided by reason, professional integrity and respect for constitutional processes,” CJI Surya Kant said in his address at the Constitutional Day celebrations organised by the Supreme Court Bar Association.

    “If political agendas are masqueraded as professional duty or judicial platforms are targeted for personal gains – there stands the professional integrity impaired,” he added.

    Why is November 26 celebrated as Constitution Day in India

    Constitution Day, also known as Samvidhan Diwas, is observed every year on November 26 to commemorate the adoption of the Constitution of India in 1949 by the Constituent Assembly.

    The Constitution came into force on January 26, 1950, two months later, making India a republic. However, November 26 is celebrated as the Constitution Day to commemorate the moment the Constitution of India was formally adopted for “We, the People of India”.
