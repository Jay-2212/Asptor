PM Modi speaks to flydubai pilot Captain Smit Machchaar

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-10-02

Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke to Flydubai pilot Captain Smit Machchhar over a video call on Friday (October 2, 2026) and lauded him for foiling an attempt to crash an Israel-bound flight. Mr. Modi called the pilot a hero who proved to the world that Indians “are willing to risk their lives to save others”.

“Today is Gandhi Jayanti, you have shown the world what the true meaning of ‘ahimsa’ (non-violence) is,” Prime Minister Modi told Captain Machchar. The pilot called Mr. Modi his “biggest idol”.

Captain Machchhar is currently hospitalised in the UAE, recovering from wounds he received when his co-pilot attacked him in an alleged bid to seize control of the aircraft that was going to Tel Aviv. A wounded Captain Machchhar opened the cockpit door, following which passengers overpowered the co-pilot. The plane later landed safely in Saudi Arabia after two other pilots on board took control.

The flight was carrying 174 passengers, including 27 children. Investigations into the incident are still on.

Captain Smit Machchhar has shown the world the highest form of courage.

Without fearing for his own life, he fought valiantly and prevented another 9/11 like attack from happening.

Speaking about his ordeal with Prime Minister Modi, Captain Machchhar said that during the scuffle with his co-pilot, his 17 years of flying made him realise the grim fate awaiting the passengers, if he failed to act. In turns emotional, he said, “I was lying on the floor (of the cockpit) injured. I was still getting hit, but realised that the plane was losing altitude. I told myself to give it one last try and open the door, because although I was thinking of saving myself, I could not bear to think of what would happen to others if I didn’t act. With whatever energy was left in me, I managed to act and open the door,” adding that passengers thereafter saved the day.

Captain Machchhar added that he always recites the Hanuman Chalisa whenever he is in difficulty, as he did that day.

Lauding the pilot’s bravery, presence of mind, and composure, the Prime Minister said Captain Machchhar took the decision in a fraction of a second and saved the lives of so many people.

“After this incident, the country can proudly say that Indians are not those who take lives, but those who are willing to risk their lives to save others,” Prime Minister Modi said. He said that had Captain Machchhar not acted, fingers would have been raised against Indians, and Indian pilots and their safety records. “You have truly done a great thing,” he said.

“At that time, you received so many injuries and were engaged in a fight of life and death. But you ignored your own safety and were concerned about the safety of the passengers. You have enhanced the honour of 140 crore Indians. I feel proud from the bottom of my heart,” Prime Minister Modi said.

He informed Captain Machchhar that temples across the country had conducted special prayers for his speedy recovery.

“You are a brave man. India is waiting to welcome you, you have enhanced the honour of the country,” he told the pilot.

Prime Minister Modi also spoke about the pilot’s mother, Geeta Ben, and said she has raised him with the values and teachings of the Bhagavad Gita. “Today is Gandhi Jayanti. You have shown the world what the true meaning of ‘ahimsa’ is,” Mr. Modi said.

The flydubai flight FZ1073, travelling from Dubai to Tel Aviv, had transmitted emergency signals on Wednesday and made a sharp descent of more than 17,000 feet from 34,000 feet before diverting to Prince Sultan Bin Abdulaziz Airport in Saudi Arabia’s Tabuk city.
