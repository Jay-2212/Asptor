Haripad school launches ‘Vidya Bank’ for hands-on banking lessons

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-09-18

In today’s world, children learn about savings and how to manage their money in school. But what if the world of banking is no longer a daunting theoretical lesson and instead becomes an experience that stays with them forever.

At Government Upper Primary School, Vellamkulangara, a rural school near Haripad in Alappuzha district, a student-run temporary bank, named Vidya Bank, has been started by the institution’s Social Science Club to provide students with experiential learning of the banking system.

As a practical extension of the chapter “Let’s Save for the Future” in the Social Science textbook for Class 6 students, V. Rajaneesh, a teacher and the convener of the Social Science Club, along with his students started the experimental banking facility to make students familiar with banking practices and principles.

Vidya Bank operates like a typical bank, with students taking roles such as cashier and manager, handling tasks like account opening and cash management. It functions from a hall on the first floor and is equipped with a bank board and designated counters for its operations..

Forms for opening accounts, KYC updates, deposits and withdrawal have been prepared under the name of Vidya Bank in the same format as found in banks, but with minor tweaks to make it easier for students to fill them in. Even account passbooks have been provided to students.

Accounts can be opened by submitting a copy of the students’ Aadhaar card or two photos and a filled-up KYC form. Teachers help pre-primary and lower primary students with the forms, while upper primary students do it on their own. The accounts can be opened as zero-balance accounts.

All 115 students from pre-primary to class VII and teachers can open accounts and conduct cash transactions on all working days from 9.15 a.m. to 9.45 a.m. and 1.30 p.m. to 2 p.m. at Vidya Bank.

Operations of bank manager, cashier, counters, customer care are handled by Class VII students who learnt about banking in Class VI and can guide the current Class VI students.

The school plans to operate Vidya Bank for three months to help students become familiar with its operations. Students can open fixed deposits for the entirety of that period, in which case an annual interest of 4% is offered to them.

For the convenience of customers, a schedule assigning specific days to different classes for conducting transactions has been prepared.

The formal launch of the bank was held on Wednesday. Grama panchayat president P. Omana and Haripad district educational officer C. Madhu performed the inauguration by handing over the account opening forms to the students. Headmistress P.S. Sindhukumari has provided support to the initiative, besides other staff members and the school’s management and development committees.

Mr. Rajaneesh who has been working at the school for 12 years says the students’ response has been very positive. They are excited about learning about banking and practically handling operations and doing transactions on their own.
