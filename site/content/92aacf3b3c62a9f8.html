    We will ensure people of J&K are taken care of: Nirmala Sitharaman

    Replying to questions raised on the Appropriation (No. 3) Bill, 2026, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said in the Rajya Sabha on August 6, 2026, that during the period when J&K was under central administration after the abrogation of Article 370 in 2019—until an elected government took office—the Centre cleared many “overhangs that J&K was suffering from.” 

    Expressing gratitude to the Prime Minister and Home Minister for their guidance, Ms. Sitharaman noted that significant time was spent studying granular details, emphasising: “I am not patronising it. We have done it because J&K is an integral part of India, including those parts occupied by Pakistan. We will ensure that the people of J&K are taken care of because we are all part of one country.” 

    She added that the central government restructured J&K’s debt and has granted an “additional ₹5,000 crore over and above the revised budget” to the Union Territory.

    The Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026, passed in Lok Sabha

    The Bill further amends the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, the Income-tax Act, 2025, and the Finance Act, 2026.

    In particular, the main changes have to do with extending existing tax holidays for companies such as Apple that supply machinery and tooling to Indian factories, providing certainty and reducing red tape for foreign fund managers looking to relocate to India, and easing the process for foreign companies to set up data centres in India.

    Rajya Sabha MP Manoj Jha raises issue of SC, ST, OBC representation in higher judiciary

    Rajya Sabha MP Manoj Jha asked the govt. anout the steps taken to ensure the wider representation of judges from ST and SC communites in the higher judiciary.

    Minister of State for Law and Justice Arjun Ram Meghwal replied that govt always sends it recommendations to include judges from ST & SC communities to Chief Justice of High Courts. He also added that Memorandum of Procedure (MoP) has provisions for ensuring the wider representation of judges from SC, ST, OBC communities in the judiciary.

    Memorandum of Procedure (MoP) provides the procedure for appointment and transfer of judges to the Supreme Court and State High Courts.
