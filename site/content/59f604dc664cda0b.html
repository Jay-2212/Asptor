    A designated delegation of the Student protestors in Jharkhand left for the Circuit House for dialogue with representatives of the Jharkhand Government regarding the demands of the aspirants.

    “The team has told us that we will go for a dialogue at the Circuit House at 10 a.m. Had the government agreed to our demands when the other students met them, then it wouldn’t have been necessary for us to go as well. It doesn’t matter how many of us go to meet them. All of us have the same demands, which should be fulfilled,” said Student leader Devendra Nath Mahto.- ANI

    Jharkhand CID summons three JPSC members on alleged govt. exam irregularities

    Jharkhand State Crime Investigation Department (CID) probing the case of alleged irregularities in the examination has now served notice to three serving members of Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC). The investigating agency has issued summons to three current JPSC members—Ajita Bhattacharya, Anima Hansda, and Jamal Ahmed and asked them to appear before the agency on August 10, 12, and 14, respectively. A crucial aspect of the investigation focuses on which grounds TSR Data Processing Private Limited (TDPL) —the agency that conducted the examination—was empanelled and selected, despite having been previously blacklisted.- Amit Bhelari
