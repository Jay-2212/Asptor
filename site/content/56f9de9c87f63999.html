    Putin says BRICS not against anyone

    Russian President Vladimir Putin stressed the BRICS grouping was not against anyone and Moscow was open to working with the whole world as he agreed to further strengthen bilateral ties with India and slammed the economic restrictions imposed by the West on his country.

    Addressing a gathering of BRICS business leaders ahead of the summit, Mr. Putin said Russia was ready to work with partners on infrastructure and logistics projects, including the Arctic Transport Corridor and North-South Transport Corridor, as well as cooperation in technology, artificial intelligence, unmanned transport, finance, tourism and industry.

    “We are not against anyone,” Mr. Putin said. “We are in favour of our interests,” while asserting that BRICS remained open to cooperation with the wider world.

    Mr. Putin said the proposed NDB-based investment platform would seek to mobilise private-sector funding and make BRICS economies more competitive. He expressed hope that other BRICS members would support the initiative.
