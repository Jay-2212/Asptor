At least 11 killed, several injured in explosion at firecracker factory in U.P.’s Kaushambi

By Mayank Kumar • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-09-01

A massive explosion at a firecracker factory in Kaushambi district on Monday (August 31, 2026) killed at least 11 people and left several injured, officials said. The blast occurred under the Pipri police station limits, after which police, Fire and Emergency Services, and Indian Air Force personnel stationed nearby launched a joint rescue operation.

“Information was received about an explosion at a firecracker factory near Manori power house in the Pipri police station area. Police and fire brigade teams immediately reached the spot. The fire brigade brought the blaze under control, and rescue operations are underway to evacuate those trapped inside the factory,” Kaushambi District Magistrate Amit Pal said.

People gather after a massive explosion at a firecracker factory in Kaushambi, Uttar Pradesh, on August 31, 2026. | Photo Credit: PTI

The fire occurred between 11.30 a.m. and noon, eyewitnesses said. Seven injured individuals were rescued from the site in a serious condition and referred to SRN Hospital in Prayagraj for advanced treatment. 

The fire also damaged nearby structures, with at least four houses located adjacent to the factory collapsing due to the impact of the explosion, officials said. 

“Rescue operations are underway on a war footing. JCBs (heavy construction equipment) and dog squads are being used to rescue people from beneath the mounds of rubble,” another official said. 

“On receiving information about the fire in the firecracker factory, the local police and fire service team immediately reached the spot and controlled the situation by carrying out necessary relief and rescue operations. Senior officials are present at the spot, and necessary action is being ensured,” the Kaushambi police said in a statement. 

Smoke erupts from charred remains after an explosion in a firecracker factory in the Pipri area, Kaushambi, on August 31, 2026. | Photo Credit: PTI

Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath expressed grief over the incident and directed officials to provide all possible assistance.

“The loss of life in the unfortunate incident that took place in Kaushambi district is extremely sad and heart-wrenching. My condolences are with the bereaved family. Local authorities have been instructed to ensure proper treatment for the injured and to contact the families of the victims and provide them with all possible assistance. Furthermore, financial assistance of ₹2 lakh each has been provided to the families of those who died in this accident and ₹50,000 each to the injured. We pray to Lord Shri Ram to give place to the departed souls in his feet and provide speedy recovery to the injured,” Mr. Adityanath posted on social media platform X. 

Samajwadi Party (SP) president Akhilesh Yadav urged the State government to investigate the causes of the blast. “The news of the deaths in the explosions at a firecracker factory, near the highly sensitive Air Force Central Command Headquarters in Kaushambi, Uttar Pradesh, is deeply saddening. Tributes. The government should provide the best possible treatment to the injured, and investigate the causes of the blast to find out whether the accident was due to someone’s negligence or the disregard of safety standards, so that such accidents are avoided in the future,” Mr. Yadav said.
