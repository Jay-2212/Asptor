    If you want good services, you need to pay for it: Nitin Gadkari

    On a question on charges at toll plazas by DMK’s P. Wilson, Mr. Gadkari elaborated on the FASTag or Unified Payments Interface (UPI) process by the government. He said the government is looking to change the whole system by eliminating toll nakas and enuring a smooth process.

    “If you want good services, you need to pay. Everything cannot be provided for free,” he said, in his answer.

    Under the National Highways Fee Rules, vehicles entering a toll plaza without a valid and functional FASTag are charged twice the applicable user fee if they pay in cash. However, highway users who choose to pay via UPI are charged 1.25 times the user fee for their respective vehicle category.

    Later, Chairperson Radhakrishnan makes a request to the Union Minister, urging him to make provisions of a pass to former MPs to travel without toll on the highways, similar to the one provided by the Railways.

    AAP's Sanjay Singh moves Rule 267 notice in Rajya Sabha against mandatory E20 fuel

    Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) MP Sanjay Singh on Wednesday (August 5, 2026) submitted a notice under Rule 267 in the Rajya Sabha, seeking a discussion on the public outrage and consumer rights issues arising from the mandatory implementation of E20 petrol ahead of the Monsoon Session in Parliament.

    “The government has advanced its ethanol-blending target from 2030 to 2025 and has effectively made E20 petrol the default fuel available at a large number of retail outlets (petrol pumps) nationwide. Consequently, millions of citizens are being compelled to use a fuel for which their vehicles were neither designed nor certified, all without any meaningful alternative or informed consent,” he said.

    He noted that the Road Transport and Highways Minister Nitin Gadkari admitted in Parliament that E20 petrol results in a 2-6% reduction in fuel efficiency (mileage), depending on the vehicle type and age.

    “This admission is significant as it establishes that consumers are forced to purchase more fuel to cover the same distance. Lakhs of vehicle owners across the country have reported a noticeable drop in mileage, a decline in engine performance, increased maintenance costs, and concerns regarding long-term engine durability. These apprehensions are no longer merely hypothetical,” he said.

    Manickam Tagore seeks debate on Jantar Mantar police action

    Congress MP Manickam Tagore B also submitted an adjournment motion notice in the Lok Sabha, seeking a discussion on the police action during a student protest held at Jantar Mantar in New Delhi on July 20 and demanding a statement from Union Home Minister Amit Shah on the incident.

These allegations require a full, fair and transparent examination,” the notice reads.
