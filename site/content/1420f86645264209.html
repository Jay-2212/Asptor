Thiruvonam Bumper lottery results 2026: First prize ₹30 crore; check date and time

By The Hindu Bureau • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-09-26

The much-anticipated draw of the Thiruvonam bumper lottery (BR-111), which offers a first prize of ₹30 crore, will be held on Saturday (September 26, 2026) at Gorkhi Bhavan in Thiruvananthapuram.

The sale of Thiruvonam bumper tickets began on July 20, and the tickets have been released in 10 series: TA, TB, TC, TD, TE, TG, TH, TJ, TK and TL. The ticket price is 500 rupees. Around 90 lakh tickets were printed for Thiruvonam Bumper 2026. Of these, 89.85 lakh tickets were sold out.

The State Lotteries Directorate had announced the hike in the first prize from ₹25 crore to ₹30 crore on July 8. The Keralam government had raised the first prize of the Thiruvonam Bumper lottery to ₹25 crore in July 2022. This is the first time in the history of the Kerala state lottery that the first prize has been increased to ₹30 crore.

Here is the official draw schedule and prize details for everyone waiting for the 2026 Thiruvonam Bumper (BR-111) results.

Kerala Thiruvonam Bumper 2026: Draw Schedule and where to watch

The draw for the 2026 Thiruvonam Bumper (BR-111) will take place at 2:00 p.m. on Saturday (September 26), and you can watch it live on the Kerala State Lottery Official YouTube Channel or track the latest results on the Kerala State Lotteries Portal.

What are the Prizes for BR-111?

The prize structure for Thiruvonam Bumper is as follows:

First prize: ₹30 crore

Second prize: ₹1 crore each to two winners each in the 20 series

Third prize: ₹25 lakh to 20 winners – two in each series

Fourth prize: ₹5 lakh to 10 winners – one per series

Fifth prize: ₹2 lakh each to 10 winners – one per series

Sixth prize: ₹5,000 to 54,000 winners

Seventh prize: ₹2,000 to 81,000 winners

Eighth prize: ₹1,000 to 1,24,000 winners

Ninth prize: ₹500 to 2,75,400 winners

How to claim the prize?

To claim prizes, winners need to check their ticket against the official Kerala Lottery results. To secure the payouts, the winning ticket must be submitted at the bank within a 30-day window following the announcement date.

The ticket holders should comply with all the protocols and submit their physical tickets along with all the required identification forms.
