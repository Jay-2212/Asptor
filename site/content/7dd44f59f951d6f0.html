Malaysia to end visa-free entry to Indians from January 1, 2027

By PTI • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-19

Malaysia is ending its visa-free access for Indian nationals from January 1, 2027, the Immigration Department of Malaysia has announced.

Earlier, Indian nationals travelling to the South East Asian nation were granted a 30-day visa exemption and just had to register for Malaysia Digital Arrival Card within three days before arrival.

The visa exemption was introduced on December 1, 2023, and later extended to the end of 2026.

The Immigration Department of Malaysia (JIM), in a notice on November 17, said the visa exemption facility for Indian nationals will no longer be available with effect from 1 January 2027.

Accordingly, Indian nationals entering Malaysia from this date onwards will be required to obtain a valid visa prior to their arrival, it said. Visa applications may be submitted through the MyVISA portal starting 1 December 2026, it added.

The JIM further said that visa processing fees would be non-refundable, regardless of the application outcome, change of policy or any subsequent changes to travel plans.

The Immigration Department of Malaysia reserves the right to amend these entry requirements at any time in accordance with directives from the Government of Malaysia, the notice said.

It also advised visitors to obtain travel medical coverage for the full duration of their intended stay in Malaysia. Medical treatment and related healthcare services are chargeable to non-residents, who are responsible for all expenses incurred, it said.

In September, India had advised its nationals in Malaysia not to use the country's 30-day visa-free entry facility to seek employment.

In an advisory posted on X, the High Commission of India in Kuala Lumpur said it has noticed a “noticeable increase” in Indian nationals arriving in Malaysia who are "misled by unscrupulous agents with false job promises." The High Commission said the 30-day visa-free facility is meant for tourism and social visits and does not permit employment.
