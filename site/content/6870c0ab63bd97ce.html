    JMM stages protest in front of Lok Bhavan, seeks CEC Gyanesh Kumar’s resignation

    The Jharkhand Mukti Morcha (JMM) staged a demonstration in front of the Lok Bhavan in Ranchi on Wednesday (September 30, 2026), seeking Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar’s resignation over alleged flaws in the pan-India Special Intensive Revision (SIR) process and demanding elections using ballot papers instead of EVMs.

    The protest march from the Ranchi College Ground to the Lok Bhavan came on a day INDIA bloc leaders met in the national capital and finalised a joint action plan over the SIR row, including holding rallies and ‘save democracy marches’. The group demanded reverting to ballots for elections instead of using EVMs.

    In the Jharkhand capital, JMM leader and workers raised slogans such as ‘Vote Chor, Gaddi Chhod‘ and ‘Shame on Gyanesh Kumar’ at the gathering in front of the Lok Bhavan. “We are protesting today to save the country’s Constitution and democracy. We demand that elections be conducted through ballot paper because no electronic machine is tamper-proof. This protest is also against the flaws in the SIR process and demands the resignation of the CEC,” said JMM’s Central General Secretary and Chief Spokesperson Supriyo Bhattacharya.

    JMM Rajya Sabha MP Mahua Maji claimed that people are scared that their names will be removed from the voter list through the SIR of electoral rolls and they would be deprived of government scheme benefits. - PTI

    Left parties to hold protest rally in Kolkata on Oct 2 seeking Gyanesh Kumar's removal as CEC

    Left parties will hold a protest rally Kolkata on October 2, demanding the removal of Gyanesh Kumar as the chief election commissioner and an immediate halt to the SIR exercise, CPI(M) West Bengal state secretary Mohammed Salim said on Wednesday.

    He condemned Tuesday’s detention of CPI(M) general secretary M.A. Baby and other Left leaders during their protest at the Election Commission headquarters in New Delhi to demand Kumar’s resignation amid the ongoing controversy over the special intensive revision (SIR) of electoral rolls.

    “A procession will be taken out from the Netaji statue to Mahatma Gandhi’s statue in the Esplanade area on October 2 to demand the removal of Gyanesh Kumar as the CEC and an immediate halt to the ongoing SIR process,” Mr. Salim said in a statement.

    INDIA bloc charts joint action plan against CEC, SIR 'irregularities'; demands return to ballot

    The INDIA bloc on Wednesday finalised a joint action plan over the SIR row, including holding rallies and ‘save democracy marches’, with Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge asserting that all parties agreed that elections should be held through ballot papers instead of EVMs.
