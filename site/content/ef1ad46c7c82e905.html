    Nepal PM Balen Shah should focus on bridging trust deficit with India: Former Nepal FM Gyawali

    Former Nepal Foreign Minister Pradeep Kumar Gyawali has said that Prime Minister Balen Shah’s maiden visit to India should focus on addressing the trust deficit between the two neighbouring countries and resolving long-pending bilateral issues.

    He also expressed concern over the lack of consultations with political parties and the President ahead of the visit, describing the approach as unprecedented.

    Speaking to ANI on Sunday, Mr. Gyawali said, “He has not held any consultations with political parties, and he has not even informed the President about this state visit. This is an unprecedented approach... We hope that he will raise issues of concern to Nepal. Most importantly, since the events of last September—followed by new elections and the formation of a new government—a sense of distance and a ‘trust deficit’ have emerged between Delhi and Kathmandu. Therefore, he should focus on bridging this gap and restoring trust between these two vital neighbouring partner nations.”
