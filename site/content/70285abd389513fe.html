No action against CJP protesters: Delhi government

• THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-07-30

 

The Home Department of the Delhi government on Thursday (July 30, 2026) issued an order that no adverse legal action will be taken against people involved in the protests against the NEET-UG paper leak issue.

“The protection, however, shall not extend to persons having criminal antecedents, as mandated in the order of Hon’ble Supreme Court mentioned above,” it said, citing a verdict dated July 28.

If anyone has been arrested or detained in cases related to the protests, the process for reviewing the arrests and releasing the arrested persons will be undertaken “expeditiously”, as per the order. 

“Government of NCT of Delhi does not propose to take any further adverse action against the individuals involved in the protests, and the matter shall be treated as closed without any future proceedings on this account,” the order said. 

However, Delhi Home Minister Ashish Sood clarified in a statement that this relief will not apply to individuals who already have criminal cases registered against them or those who indulged in violence, vandalism, or any other unlawful activities under the guise of the protest. 

“Such persons will face action strictly in accordance with the law. The government fully respects the democratic right to peaceful expression, but no leniency will be shown towards those who disrupt law and order or engage in violent activities,” Mr. Sood said. 

The CJP had said that the government’s agreement was to withdraw cases against “all protesters” and not take any action against them in the future in relation to the protests. 

On Saturday (July 25, 2026), the CJP called off its 36-day protest after the Central government agreed to its three main demands, including the resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan as Education Minister. The other two demands were withdrawal of cases against all protesters and no future action against them, as well as compensation to families whose children died by suicide due to the NEET paper leak. 

On Monday (July 27, 2026) the CJP warned of fresh protests, accusing the Centre of violating its assurance that no punitive action would be taken against protesters. Later in the evening, the CJP said that West Bengal, Assam and Bihar had announced to withdraw FIRs.
