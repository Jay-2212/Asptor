No power can harm India as long as it reveres Sikh Gurus, says U.P. CM Adityanath

By Mayank Kumar • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-25

Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath on Tuesday (November 24, 2026) said that no power in the world will be able to harm India as long as it continues to hold Sikh Gurus in reverence and respect.

“The sacrifice, penance, martyrdom, and spiritual consciousness of the Sikh Gurus are invaluable treasures of India’s national unity, social harmony, and cultural pride,” Mr. Adityanath said, addressing a program organised on the occasion of the 557th Prakash Parv of Sahib Shri Guru Nanak Dev Ji in Lucknow .

“Through ‘Ek Onkar’, Guru Nanak Dev Ji Maharaj gave the message of uniting all humanity. His teachings remain as relevant today as they were then. We must not confine these teachings to events and celebrations alone, but imbibe them in our lives and continuously familiarize the younger generation with them as well,” he added.

Referring to the spiritual and religious significance of Kartik Purnima, the U.P. Chief Minister said, “Today is an extremely sacred day for entire state. Since 4 am, I have been receiving updates on the number of devotees and arrangements at various religious sites across the state. At Shukratal in Muzaffarnagar, lakhs of devotees are taking a holy dip in the Ganga.

At Garhmukteshwar in Hapur, approximately 15 lakh devotees took a holy dip on Tuesday. Meanhwhile, more than 15 lakh devotees took a dip of faith in the Ganga at Tigri in Amroha. A large number of devotees also arrived in Prayagraj and Farrukhabad, while lakhs of them visited the Ganga in Ayodhya and Kashi,”.

Referring to the journeys undertaken by Guru Nanak Dev Ji and social conditions prevailing during his time, Mr. Adityanath said, “When he conveyed the message of unity to humanity, society was grappling with numerous social evils and divisions. Society was fragmented in the name of caste, untouchability and discrimination.

At the same time, he said foreign invasions and external attacks posed challenges. During such difficult times, Guru Nanak Dev Ji Maharaj united all humanity through the mantra of ‘Ek Onkar’. His clear message was that God is one and we are all His children. Therefore, every human being deserves equal respect. This mantra, given by him, became a great force for uniting society at that time, and it remains relevant even today. Despite limited means and resources, Guru Nanak Dev Ji Maharaj traveled to various places and spread the message of humanity, equality and spiritual consciousness among the masses,”.

Explaining the three principal teachings of Guru Nanak Dev Ji Maharaj, ‘Naam Japo, Kirat Karo, Vand Chhako’, the U.P. CM added, “Remembering God, performing kirtan and embracing the spirit of togetherness generate positive feelings within human beings. This dispels ignorance and awakens the strength of unity in society,”..
