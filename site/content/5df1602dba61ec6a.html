Break free of limiting beliefs to achieve new heights: PM Modi at IIT Kharagpur

By Shrabana Chatterjee • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-21

Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Saturday (November 21, 2026) said that if people go ahead with the “nation first” mindset, then new opportunities will come their way. He asked students to break free of limiting beliefs to achieve new heights, at the Platinum jubilee celebrations of the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur.

“This place has always come forward in building the nation at every stage of the country since its independence,” Mr. Modi said while addressing the students at the 75th anniversary celebrations at IIT Kharagpur, which is also the first IIT of independent India. He also hoped that the institute will continue to make similar contributions in building the future of the nation.

“We have limiting beliefs; we limit our thoughts, our dreams, our aspirations, our talent, and give up. But we only reach our full potential if we break free of the limits to unlock new possibilities,” Mr. Modi said. He added that people put limit to their own thoughts, restricting the opportunities in their lives and of the nation.

Mr Modi said that during his tenure as the PM, he has broken the limits and achieved many such goals which people believed to be “impossible”.

“Poverty in India was known to be a permanent problem. But there was no real change made through policy; they only made files. People took the situation for granted. But when we came to power, we changed this mentality and empowered the poor and started schemes to get them out of poverty,” Mr. Modi said. He added that according to a World Bank report, in the last 12-13 years at least 50 crore people in India have been taken out of poverty.

Mr. Modi added that in the coming days, when India is going forward towards a “Viksit Bharat”, IIT Kharagpur is sure to play a major role in building that dream. The PM further said that he hopes the 100 years of IIT Kharagpur will be celebrated in the Vikshit Bharat in the coming years.

West Bengal Governor R.N. Ravi, Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari and Higher Education Minister Jagannath Chattopadhyay were among the others present at the 75-year program.

Meanwhile, the director of the institute took the stage and said that under the PM’s leadership, they are now remaining true to the mission of IIT Kharagpur. “From grassroots to skyrockets, that is the new IIT Kharagpur,” Professor Suman Chakraborty said. He also added that IIT Kharagpur alumni and Google CEO Sundar Pichai have offered a 5 million dollar support for applied Artificial Intelligence research.
