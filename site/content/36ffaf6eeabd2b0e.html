CJP warns of ‘Jantar Mantar 2.0’ if CEC Gyanesh Kumar does not resign

By Suruchi Kumari • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-09-24

The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) on Thursday (September 24, 2026) demanded the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar for “undermining” voter rights through the ongoing Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. The group warned of a nationwide agitation and “Jantar Mantar 2.0” if he did not step down within 48 hours.

The CJP’s demand for CEC Gyanesh Kumar’s resignation comes a day after an investigation by The Indian Express revealed that the other two Election Commissioners, Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi, objected on record at least 14 times between October 2025 and August 2026 to decisions related to SIR, many of which, they said, were taken without their knowledge.

“To ensure free and fair elections, CEC Gyanesh Kumar must resign,” CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke said at a press conference. He said that if Mr. Kumar didn’t resign in two days, the outfit would lead a sit-in like the one at Jantar Mantar in June and campaigns all over the country.

Outside the venue, NM centre in New Delhi, where the group gathered, over a dozen police personnel had been deployed.

Mr. Dipke alleged that the electoral process had been ‘rigged’ and that the right to vote was being taken away from citizens, for which the CEC was responsible.

Mr. Dipke also said that Opposition parties should take a stand against election rigging. He said, “If you know the election is rigged, you should take a stand; you cannot both participate in the election and speak against rigging.”

CJP co-convenor Saurav Das said, “Everywhere we are going as part of our ‘School Theek Karo’ campaign, people are upset and asking for a change. One of the important tools for change is our right to vote. It cannot be compromised.”

CJP’s three demands

Besides the call for the resignation of Mr. Kumar within 48 hours, the CJP also made two other demands, including criminal proceedings against the CEC. “Criminal proceedings should be initiated against Gyanesh Kumar immediately,” the outfit’s co-convenor Ashutosh Ranka said.

Mr. Ranka further said, “An independent inquiry commission should be constituted to look into all proceedings of SIR so far. The Supreme Court should seize all files related to decision-making in SIR before they are destroyed by EC.”

He also demanded that all the upcoming elections be halted and pre-SIR rolls of January 2025 restored in all States.

The CJP’s third demand is to repeal the Appointment of Chief Election Commissioner and Other Election Commissioners Act 2023. “The new law should be re-drawn with active participation of civil society, an independent selection process that inspires public confidence,” Mr. Ranka said.

“We need to hit the streets. Who is Gyanesh Kumar to revoke our voting rights and strike our names off?” he said.

‘Biggest threat to democracy’

Mr. Dipke called the CEC a threat to India’s democracy, citing that the ruling party had won in more States than it did during the peak of ‘Modi wave’. “He should resign immediately because he is the biggest threat to India’s democracy. What did democracy mean in our country? Democracy meant that people elected the government. Since Gyanesh Kumar became the Chief Election Commissioner, the government has been deciding who will be allowed to vote and who will not. Is this democracy?” he asked.

Mr. Das, referring to the report, said, “The other two Election Commissioners clearly called this illegal, but even then, the Election Commission of India, under the direction of Gyanesh Kumar and whoever is directing or controlling him has continued carrying out these illegal actions for months. It has been going on for a long time, for nearly one and a half years, since the SIR began.”
