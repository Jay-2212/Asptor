    SC calls the row a 'brazen and mutual disobedience' of the court's order

    The Thirupparankundram lamplighting litigation has a chequered history, the Supreme Court notes in the order.

    “In the light of prohibitory orders issued by the DM under S.163 BNSS, certain devotees had moved the Madras HC. The District Administration led by Collector had given an undertaking to comply with the Supreme Court November 20 order. Despite this, the authorities allegedly colluded with temple adminsitration to light the lamp at the Uchipillaiyar temple.

Devasthanam board purportedly overreached the order of the court by giving a strained interpretation of the November 20 order. The Collector is taking umbrage under a resolution of the devasthanam,” the apex court said.
