    INDIA bloc leaders rally behind Neha after detention during Delhi protest

    INDIA bloc leaders on Sunday rallied behind AISA national president Neha after she was picked up by police while attempting to march towards Jantar Mantar and allegedly taken away in a private vehicle without an official number plate, with her whereabouts initially not known to her colleagues and supporters.

    AISA members alleged that no woman police officer was present in the vehicle and said they were unable to contact Neha after she was taken away.

    All India Democratic Women’s Association (AIDWA) leaders Asha Sharma and Maimoona Mollah, RTI activist Anjali Bharadwaj, activist Navsharan Singh and other senior leaders reached Parliament Street Police Station seeking information about Neha and other women activists who had allegedly been taken away in unmarked private cars. Neha was later released from Narela Industrial Area police station.

    Reacting to her release, TMC Rajya Sabha leader Derek O’Brien said he had been anxious after learning that Neha had been taken away. -- PTI

    Press Club condemns police action on journalists at anti-CEC protests

    The Press Club of India on Sunday (October 4, 2026) condemned the Delhi Police’s alleged treatment of journalists covering protests against Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar at Jantar Mantar and demanded immediate action on complaints of sexual harassment against police personnel.

    A statement from the Press Club came a day after three women journalists alleged sexual harassment by police personnel while covering the protest on Saturday. The Delhi Police said it has received their complaints and transferred those to the Crime Branch for a “fair and impartial enquiry”.

    “The Press Club of India is shocked and dismayed at the Delhi Police’s actions against and treatment of journalists during the protests at Jantar Mantar against the chief election commissioner on Friday and Saturday,” the journalists’ body said in the statement.

    It said a woman journalist has alleged that she was “molested and groped by an ACP” and spent hours at the Parliament Street police station on Saturday night trying to get an FIR registered. -- PTI

    Street anarchy cannot hijack democracy: BJP seeks to debunk Opposition claims on SIR

    Amid protests against SIR and alleged “vote theft”, the BJP on Sunday (October 4, 2026) said that the names of 13 crore voters have not been deleted as claimed by the Congress but flagged in the draft electoral roll under categories such as dead, absent, shifted or duplicate.

    A “final action” on this follows claims, objections and appeals, the ruling party said on X from its official handle, while posting a “point-by-point breakdown of the claims and facts behind” the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls and accusing the Congress of running a “fear campaign” against SIR.

    Rebutting another claim of the opposition, the BJP asserted that Form 6 was not changed and that an additional declaration was attached for voter convenience.

    “Thirteen crore names were not deleted. They were flagged in the draft under categories such as dead, absent, shifted or duplicate. Final action follows claims, objections and appeals,” It said.

    “Form 6 was not changed. An additional declaration was attached for voter convenience. Electoral rolls remain decentralised, with State Election machinery retaining statutory powers,” it added. -- PTI

    Gyanesh Kumar has lost moral right to remain CEC, says Congress

    The Congress on Sunday (October 4, 2026) cited Supreme Court judge Justice Ujjal Bhuyan’s reported remarks on right to vote and claimed that his comments conclusively prove that the CEC has violated the Constitution both in letter and spirit.

    The opposition party said Justice Bhuyan’s comment has exposed Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar “lock, stock and barrel”, and he has lost all moral right to continue in office.

    Congress general secretary communications Jairam Ramesh shared a media report quoting Justice Bhuyan as saying if any process results in disenfranchisement of millions of Indian citizens, rendering them second class citizens in their own country -- devoid of voting rights -- such a process is intrinsically arbitrary.

    “Justice Ujjal Bhuyan, a sitting judge of the Supreme Court, has been an outspoken champion of environmental protection,” Ramesh said on X.

    Now the courageous jurist has forcefully expressed his view that the right to vote is very much part of the basic structure of the Constitution, the Congress leader said. “This conclusively proves that the CEC -- constitutional functionary himself -- has violated the Constitution itself both in letter and spirit.” -- PTI

    SC to hear on Oct 5 pleas for suspension of CEC Gyanesh Kumar

    The Supreme Court is scheduled to hear on Monday (October 5, 2026) a batch of pleas seeking suspension of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar and challenging the manner in which the poll panel led by him took decisions, allegedly without the approval of two election commissioners.

    A bench of Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana is scheduled to hear the pleas filed by Rakesh Kumar Singh and advocate Shailendra Mani Tripathi, who have also challenged the decisions, guidelines and software changes allegedly made by Kumar without the concurrence of his two colleagues.

    The PIL, filed by Patna resident Rakesh Kumar Singh, has urged the top court to determine whether Kumar has been individually exercising powers that legally belong to the Election Commission (EC) as a whole, and, if so, to declare those actions unauthorised and take appropriate legal action. -- PTI
