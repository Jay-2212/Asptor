PM Modi should resign for ‘pressuring’ CEC: Kharge at CWC meet

By Sandeep Phukan • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-09-29

Alleging a “match-fixing” between Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Home Minister Amit Shah and Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar, the Congress Working Committee (CWC) on Tuesday demanded Mr. Modi’s resignation, the removal of the CEC, an immediate suspension of the special intensive revision (SIR) of electoral rolls and the restoration of 13.3 crore names deleted through the exercise.

The CWC resolution called for the resignations of Mr. Modi and Mr. Shah after party president Mallikarjun Kharge said that “Mr. Kumar was a small fish and the real big ones are the Prime Minister and the Home Minister”. The remark was strongly endorsed by Congress Parliamentary Party chairperson Sonia Gandhi.

In its resolution, the CWC said an exercise of “doubtful constitutional validity cannot be allowed to decide who may vote in the world’s largest democracy”.

“This brazen Vote Chori has been organised by the CEC on behalf of the Prime Minister and the Home Minister. Nothing short of their resignation will ensure the safety of our Constitution and hard-earned democracy,” the resolution said.

“For the Indian National Congress, the struggle to restore universal adult franchise in India is no less than the Freedom Struggle itself,” it said.

The CWC expressed “grave concern over the systematic subversion” of India’s electoral system and said the Bharatiya Janata Party and its “handpicked officials” must understand that this “brazen theft” of the people’s franchise would not go unanswered.

Addressing a joint press conference with Jairam Ramesh, Congress general secretary (organisation) K. C. Venugopal said that since the government was not listening to the people, the only way for parties that believed in democracy was to resist.

“Gyanesh Kumar is only a tool. Whenever dictators lose the confidence of common voters, they will try to find special ways to stay in power. This exercise (SIR) could not happen without the concurrence of Modi and Amit Shah,” Mr. Venugopal said.

“There is a moral responsibility for the PM on this issue. We are demanding resignation from the PM on moral grounds,” he said.

Mr. Venugopal said the INDIA bloc meeting on Wednesday would chalk out a further course of action to resist what the Congress has termed vote chori (vote theft). “The Congress wants to hold a massive agitation to save democracy in the country with the help of all political parties. There will be valuable suggestions from leaders of the INDIA bloc tomorrow; after that we will sit and formalise the clear action plan for the future agitation,” he said, adding, “We will coordinate between the INDIA bloc agitation programme and that of the Congress”.

In his opening remarks to the CWC, Mr. Kharge put forward five demands, including making the complete data on every major change made to the electoral rolls public, and providing political parties with the electoral rolls and relevant data in a simple format and in a timely manner. He also demanded an independent and time-bound inquiry wherever evidence of serious discrepancies emerges.

“We do not view this issue merely as a battle to win or lose an election,” the Congress chief said, adding, “We must convey to every citizen that in a democracy, the most powerful person is the ordinary voter — not the Prime Minister, the Chief Minister, or a leader in power.”
