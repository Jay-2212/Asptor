LeT’s Lateef Bhat killed in Kulgam encounter

By Peerzada Ashiq • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-18

Lashkar-e-Taiba terrorist Lateef Bhat, whom top police officials described as “the last local recruit” in Kashmir, was killed outside a Kulgam mosque that he had been using for shelter on Wednesday.

Security forces found Bhat, 22, after an eight-day operation led them to a mosque in Kulgam’s Zangalpora. Officials said that contact was established with the terrorist, who was hiding inside the mosque, at around 3 a.m. Security forces made him an offer of surrender, which he turned down, stating that “he was happy to meet Allah”. Bhat was later shot dead around 50 metres away from the mosque, as he made a bid to escape the cordon of the security forces.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah praised the security agencies for their joint action. “With the coordinated efforts of [Jammu and Kashmir police] JKP, Indian Army, [Central Reserve Police Force] CRPF, and intelligence agencies, Lashkar’s last known active local terrorist in the valley, Lateef Bhat, was neutralised,” he said. “One of his terrorist associates, Umar Nisar, was also neutralised in Kulgam, the day before. The entire operation was marked by a very high level of synergy,” he added.

‘Maximum restraint’

An Army spokesman said the security forces had displayed “exemplary restraint during conduct of operations, ensuring the sanctity of the religious place and neutralised the terrorist with precision by forcing him to move out of the mosque premises”.

“We had a long conversation with him on the phone. We asked him to surrender but he refused. We exercised maximum restraint given he was hiding inside a mosque,” said Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) Anayat Ali Choudhary. The police said a weapon was also recovered from the site of the operation.

LeT’s last local recruit

A college dropout and carpenter from Kulgam’s Khrewan, Bhat joined the Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) in November 2025 and became a wanted terrorist with a police reward of ₹15 lakh on his head. A week ago, Bhat attacked Central Reserve Police Force personnel in Kulgam’s Akhran area on November 11, leaving three jawans injured. In the eight days since, the security forces carried out multiple anti-terror operations in the district, killing Bhat’s accomplice Umar Nisar Dar, a baker by profession, two days ago in Kulgam’s Laroo Bypass area.

According to the police, both Bhat and Dar were behind the November 11 attack in Kulgam. Bhat was also behind the twin attacks in Anantnag and Kulgam in July, which left one policeman and two migrant workers from Chhattisgarh dead. However, he has escaped the anti-terror operations of the security forces multiple times, officials said.

“Over the past two years, we have brought local terrorist recruitment in J&K down to nearly zero. Only two Kashmiri youths had joined the LeT in these two years; Umar Nisar was neutralised in Kulgam two days ago, and the last remaining active local terrorist, Lateef Bhat, was neutralised in Kulgam today,” said Director General of Police Nalin Prabhat.

Dismantling the terror ecosystem

Remembering 1,623 police martyrs, Mr. Prabhat said, “We have been fighting Pakistan-sponsored terrorism for the past 35 years. Under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Union Home Minister Amit Shah, and Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha, we are dismantling the entire terror ecosystem.”

He said 240 accused were arrested in 2025, while 210 have been arrested so far this year under the Unlawful Activities Prevention Act (UAPA). “While 155 properties linked to terrorists were attached in 2025, 122 were attached this year,” the DGP said.

As the J&K Police claimed that Bhat was the last local LeT recruit, J&K Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha said, “J&K will soon become terror-free. Only a handful of foreign terrorists remain in the Union Territory. Terrorism remains the greatest threat to peace and progress, but sustained efforts are strengthening security and stability.”

Speaking at the Sher-i-Kashmir Police Academy in Jammu’s Udhampur, Mr. Sinha said, ”For decades, an environment was created in which shops, schools, and businesses remained closed for half the year. But under the guidance of PM Narendra Modi, the nation delivered a strong blow, and the shops of conflict entrepreneurs were shut down.”
