Government fears that its failures will be exposed internationally: Dipke

By Vinaya Deshpande Pandit • THE HINDU NATIONAL • 2026-11-25

Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) founder Abhijeet Dipke on Tuesday (November 24, 2026) slammed the Central government after its co-founder Saurav Das was stopped at the Delhi airport and denied permission to travel to the U.K.

“Vijay Mallya, Nirav Modi, and Mehul Choksi were never stopped from leaving India after committing massive scams, yet a 27-year-old legal journalist like Saurav Das was not allowed to travel when he was only going to attend a university conference (at Oxford) to speak about the CJP, and how Ambedkar and Gandhi’s philosophy shaped our movement,” Mr. Dipke told reporters at Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

Mr. Dipke alleged that the government fears that its failures will be exposed internationally if young leaders share ground realities abroad.

U.P. elections

Mr. Dipke also clarified the party’s stand on the upcoming Assembly elections in Uttar Pradesh and five other States. “The CJP will neither contest elections directly nor support any political party, but will push for elections to be fought on real youth issues such as education, healthcare, unemployment, semiconductors, renewable energy, technology, and transport, rather than religious polarisation or Mandir-Masjid politics,” he said.

Nagpur rally

Mr. Dipke and co-convener Ashutosh Ranka are expected to address a ‘Save Constitution’ rally in Nagpur on November 26 on the occasion of Constitution Day.

The CJP rally is slated to be held at Samvidhan Chowk. “We will approach all prominent personalities, social activists, representatives of organisations in Nagpur to be a part of the rally. Mr. Dipke and Mr. Ranka will speak on the values enshrined in our Constitution at the programme,” an organiser said.

“The rally will last almost two hours from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m. on November 26. Thereafter, the two leaders will address those who gather there,” an organiser from Nagpur told The Hindu. The group has so far approached Satyapal Maharaj, a prominent social educator in the State. “We will firm up the entire schedule by Wednesday,” he said.

Earlier, in a post on X, Mr. Dipke said: “This Constitution Day, let us all come together and pledge to protect our democracy, uphold the values of our Constitution, and follow the path shown to us by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar. See you all in Nagpur on November 26! #jaihind #jaibhim.” The announcement comes within days of Mr. Dipke’s visit to Goa where he targeted the BJP accusing the State government of land grab.

During the visit to Nagpur, Mr. Dipke will meet various prominent personalities, social activists, representatives of organisations, and citizens.

“Constitution Day is not merely a day for celebration; it is a day that reminds us of the values, rights, and duties enshrined in the Constitution. Therefore, the Cockroach Janta Party appeals to the citizens of Nagpur, youth, social activists, and those who believe in the Constitution, to participate in large numbers,” the party said in its appeal to the people of Nagpur.
