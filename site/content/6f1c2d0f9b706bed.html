‘We got this before GTA 6’: Why the BJP and Congress are invoking a viral meme in their political battle

The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI has given India’s political parties a new reference point in their social media messaging, with the BJP and Congress drawing on the video game’s popularity to trade political barbs.

The exchange centres on a popular internet joke about the 13-year gap between GTA V, released in 2013, and its successor, which launched worldwide on Thursday (November 19).

The BJP, including Prime Minister Narendra Modi, used the comparison to highlight what it describes as India’s progress over this period. The Congress has questioned the ruling party’s decision to associate itself with a game built around criminal activity and violence.

First, what is GTA?

First released in 1997, Grand Theft Auto is an open-world video game franchise in which players explore fictional American cities, undertake missions and engage in activities ranging from driving and exploration to theft and violent crime.

Developed by Rockstar Games, the series has become known for its sprawling virtual worlds, satirical portrayal of American society and the freedom it gives players to interact with their surroundings. Its best-known instalments include GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV and GTA V.

Players can move freely through these virtual worlds, travelling on foot or by vehicle, and undertake missions involving everything from petty theft to organised crime. The games also recreate aspects of everyday life, including phone calls and messages, while using fictional characters and businesses to satirise American society.

GTA VI follows Jason and Lucia, a couple involved in criminal activity in a fictionalised version of Florida. Like its predecessors, the game features violence and criminal missions alongside an expansive world that players can explore.

And what is the meme?

Story continues below this ad

The political exchange draws on a meme that became popular during the long wait for GTA VI: “We got [something] before GTA VI.” Internet users inserted major political events, technological developments and other milestones into the phrase to joke about how much had changed since the previous instalment.

The 13-year gap made the joke particularly adaptable to political messaging. For the BJP, it offered a way to present its account of changes in India since 2013 using a reference familiar to younger audiences. For the Congress, the game’s criminal themes provided an opportunity to ridicule the Prime Minister’s choice of imagery.

The franchise’s popularity added to the attention surrounding the exchange. Rockstar Games, the game’s publisher, said GTA VI generated $3.79 billion in retail sales on its first day, describing it as the biggest entertainment launch in history.

How has the BJP used GTA VI?

As anticipation surrounding the game’s release grew, the Prime Minister’s X and Instagram accounts published a series of slides borrowing the franchise’s distinctive typography and imagery.

“Between GTA V and VI, here’s how India upgraded”, the post stated, followed by images of ‘Level Up’ across various sectors from the economy and poverty alleviation to ramping up its solar power generation capacity and start-up ecosystem.

The campaign used the period between the two releases to showcase the government’s claims about India’s economic growth, infrastructure development and other achievements.

According to BJP sources, the party’s social media workers had identified the trend before the game’s launch. However, the decision to capitalise on it was made following Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi’s recent interaction with students at US universities.

On Saturday (November 21), Minister of Road Transport and Highways Nitin Gadkari’s social media account used similar imagery to highlight highway construction and reductions in travel time across the country.

Story continues below this ad

Several BJP state units and accounts associated with senior leaders, including Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma, also amplified the trend.

Why has the Congress objected?

The Congress attacked the Prime Minister’s choice of imagery, pointing to the game’s central themes of crime and violence.

Congress communications in-charge Jairam Ramesh criticised the Prime Minister’s post, referring to GTA VI’s protagonists, Jason and Lucia, who are involved in criminal activity. He said in a post, “The PM in a desperate bid to reach out to the youth has resorted to GTA V and GTA VI imagery on social media. Recall that this video game follows a criminal duo. How very appropriate.”

The BJP responded by accusing the Congress of focusing on the video game’s storyline rather than engaging with the figures presented in Modi’s post.

BJP social media co-convenor Priti Gandhi said the post was “about exposing Congress-era failures & showcasing India’s progress” since 2014. “You were unable to counter a single figure, so you chose to moralise about video-game characters…” she alleged.

The Congress also circulated a minute-long video highlighting the game’s violent themes, captioned “From Vote Chori To GTA: Modi’s Perfect Match”.
